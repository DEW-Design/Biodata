# 2026-10-01 - Explore drops column 2, search areas live in the card

- **Oct 1 2026: Explore drops column 2, search areas live in the card.** The designer asked to roll Explore's search areas into the floating search card because column 2 took space, and confirmed hiding column 2 on Explore so the map gets the full width. This overrides CONTRACTS 3.7 (three columns) for Explore's first layout (`/pages/observations`) only, for every persona, recorded as a scoped override in 3.7 beside the project-page one (30 Sept, decision `2026-09-30-07`); the second layout (`/pages/observations/option-2`) is unchanged, and it is not a precedent. What changed: the `Explore` column 2 (the areas list and, for roles that do not see all data, the access notice card) is gone. `AreaLayerList` (`app/pages/_shared/map-search/area-layers.tsx`) is now a section of the card under the search field, folded to one row that reads "Search areas", a count and the first area's name ("Circle 1 and 2 more"); it opens to the same layer rows (show or hide, rename, change radius, remove, zoom to, hover highlight on the map) in a list capped at 224px that scrolls inside itself, with "Clear all" beside the row when there are several. It stays open on its own while every area is hidden (the results are empty then and the way back is in the list), and the "Every area is hidden" hint now points to "Search areas" rather than "the list on the left". The data-access notice is the warning banner the second layout already uses (`dlaBanner`), across the top of the map. Choices made on the designer's behalf, from the options offered: fold and expand from a row (over chips or a popover) and the existing banner (over a card footer line); they did not choose a banner placement explicitly. The areas list is also now reachable below the `lg` breakpoint, where column 2 was hidden and the areas were not shown at all. Verified: tsc, eslint on the two files, check:contracts, and a live Playwright pass (BioData Admin and Registered User): no column 2, circle drawn, row collapsed and opened by mouse and Enter, three areas, all hidden (list held open, toggle disabled, hint shown, showing one releases it), banner and its button for the registered user, option 2 untouched, zero console errors. Open: "Search areas 1" and "0 records across 1 area" now say the same thing twice in the card (the second could drop "across N areas"); the map's auto-fit padding was left as it is (it is measured from the map, so the card still clears it).
