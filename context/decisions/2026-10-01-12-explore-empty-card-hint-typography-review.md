# 2026-10-01 - Explore empty card hint typography review

- **Oct 1 2026: Explore empty card hint typography review.** Page feedback on the empty Explore card's guidance line: "needs a typographic hierarchy review". Measured in a live browser (BioData Admin, 1708px): the card's layers are the title 16px / 600 / primary (Barlow), the Add area button 14px / 600, the search field 14px / 400 with a placeholder at rgb(181, 178, 175) (2.1:1 on white), and the hint 14px / 400 in tertiary rgb(112, 107, 104) (5.3:1). Sources per 2.9: the hint's size and colour are the design system's hint and the app's own explanatory-line pattern (the list empty state, the peek card note, both 14px tertiary), so they stay; the title is the card-heading pattern. Findings and what was done: (1) the hint wrapped to two lines and, balanced, left a 233px measure in a 366px column, so it read as stranded; the copy is now one line ("Add an area, or search a species across South Australia.", 336px), still giving both ways to start and saying what a species search with no area covers, and dropping the "type a species" that repeated the field's own placeholder; (2) the line was a free-standing paragraph although it describes the field: it is now tied to the search field with `aria-describedby` (the field's accessible description reads it), and only while it is shown; (3) the paragraph gets `m-0` so the card's own gap sets the space. Not changed, for the designer: the placeholder is the faintest text on the card at 2.1:1, darker text (the hint) sits under it, and the field is the thing to act on; the placeholder colour is the design system's input token (every field in the app), so lifting it is a token change that Figma governs, not a fix to make on this screen. Verified: tsc, eslint; live: the hint is one line, the field's accessible description is the hint, and typing a keyword removes the hint and the description; zero console errors. Open: whether to lift the input placeholder token; whether the hint is needed at all once the Add area button and the field say the same (it was kept because the designer asked for a review of it, not its removal). Not committed.
