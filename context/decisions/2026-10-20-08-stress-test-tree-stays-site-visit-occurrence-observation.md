# 2026-10-20 - Stress test tree stays Site, Visit, Occurrence, Observation: vegetation answers sit on the Visit

- **Oct 20 2026: Stress test tree stays Site, Visit, Occurrence, Observation: vegetation answers sit on the Visit.** Designer: "To still follow - Site, Visit, Occurrence, Observation style tree. That shouldn't change." Corrects decision (1) of `2026-10-20-07`, which hung a Community Observation named "Vegetation" directly under the Visit with no Occurrence above it. That is not the tree: an Observation sits under an Occurrence, and a vegetation answer has no species to be an Occurrence of.
  - **Changed.** `template-ingest.ts` no longer emits the OBSERVATIONS row. The form's vegetation answers (Veg Type, Structure, Condition, Habitat Comment) are columns of the VISIT row, as BDBSA's own VISIT tab holds them. The Visit template has no field for them yet, so the page keeps them and flags them for review in the JSON; no data is dropped and the Visit has no errors.
  - **Answers the hierarchy question left open in `2026-10-20-07`:** a Ramble form is a Site and a Visit with species under it, not a Ramble Event. Nothing in the pipeline builds any other shape.
