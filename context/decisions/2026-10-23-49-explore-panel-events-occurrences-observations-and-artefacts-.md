# 2026-10-23 - Explore panel: Events, Occurrences, Observations and Artefacts tabs removed

- **Oct 23 2026: Explore panel: Events, Occurrences, Observations and Artefacts tabs removed.** The designer: "Can we remove events, occurrences, observations and artefacts from the explore panel?" The panel now has two tabs, **Species** and **Projects**, as the redesign picture (`2026-10-23-48`) draws it.
  - **What went (all in `app/pages/observations/observations-search.tsx`, 246 lines fewer):** the four tabs and their lists; their dots on the map (the Species tab plots the occurrences that are species, the Projects tab plots projects, as before); their export branches (Projects exports its own rows to `biodata-sa-projects-search`, Species to `biodata-sa-species-search`, as before); the artefact viewer opened from a row or a dot (`ArtefactLightbox`, `resourceToArtefact` and its helpers); the observation branch of the marker click; the per-type icons and labels; and the imports only they used. `EntityTab` is now the one value `"projects"`. The saved search's `entityTab` is no longer read (Explore opens on Species whatever was saved, as it already did), so a search saved on the Events tab before this cannot open a tab that no longer exists.
  - **What stayed, on purpose:** what a search *finds*. A Project is found when anything under it (an event, an occurrence, an observation, an artefact) falls inside the search areas and matches the keyword (`matchingProjectIds`), and the species rows are the occurrences of the Projects found, so the Projects and Species lists are exactly what they were. Only the lists were removed, not the data model: `search-data.ts`, the record summary card (`RecordPeekCard` still opens for a species and a project, with "Show in project") and the other screens that use these records are untouched. The retired second layout's and the dataset ingestion lab's own copies of these tabs are not Explore's panel and were not touched.
  - **Open, for the designer: the numbers.** "Search results" at the head of the card and each area's "N records" still count every kind of record the search finds (60 in the keyword-only search: 5 projects, 19 events, 15 occurrences, 15 observations and 6 artefacts), so they no longer add up to anything the panel lists (Species 14, Projects 5). The redesign picture does the same (54 records, with Species 13 and Projects 4), so it was left as drawn. Options: keep it as "records found"; count what is listed (species plus projects); or count only the species rows. Not decided; it is a content change to a headline number.
  - **3.13:** with two tabs, icons would fit without scrolling, so the first reason for exempting Explore's tabs is gone; the redesign still draws them without icons, so the exemption stays and its reason is restated (CONTRACTS.md, `ref-shell.md`, the pattern page).
  - **Checked live (Playwright):** BioData Super Admin, Registered User and Public User all show two tabs ("Species 14 | Projects 5"; the Public User's Species count is 13, one fewer than the other two roles, as the role's access rules give); each tab's heading and Export data; a Species row and a Projects row each open their summary card with "Show in project"; the CSV export downloads on both tabs; the map shows 16 items on Species and 7 on Projects; zero console errors. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the file and `npm run check:contracts` clean. A search saved before this change was not tested (the seeded state was rejected by the store's version check, which is the storage layer's message and not the page's); the page no longer reads the saved tab at all. **Not checked:** the stacked layout below 648px, Safari, a real touch device.
