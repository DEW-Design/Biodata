# 2026-10-23 - Scrollbar on the map cards: option D (a drawn thumb), built into the shared list

- **Oct 23 2026: Scrollbar on the map cards: option D (a drawn thumb), built into the shared list.** The designer, choosing from the lab in `2026-10-23-50`: "Option D for the scroll."
  - **What D is, in the product.** The browser's scrollbar is off on a list in a map card and a thin thumb is drawn over the list's right edge: 6px wide, no track, 2px in from the edge, so it takes no width from the rows whatever the system's "Show scroll bars" setting is (the lane measured 0px on Explore's results, its areas and Map visualise's list). It shows while the list scrolls and for 0.9s after, and while the pointer is over the list or the thumb; it darkens on hover and while dragged; dragging it scrolls the list by the ratio of the heights; it stops 8px short of each end so it clears the card's rounded corner. On a touch screen it is an indicator only (`pointer-events: none`), so it never takes a tap from a row. Wheel, touch and keyboard scrolling are the browser's, as before; the rows are the Tab stops. The fade at the foot, the pinned Show N more (`2026-10-23-52`) and the contained overscroll are unchanged.
  - **Where it lives.** It is built into `ScrollFade` for `axis="y"`, the one scroller every map-card list already uses, so it needed no change at a call site: Explore's results list and Map visualise's list (through `MapResultList`) and Explore's areas list. The thumb is placed from the list's own offsets, so the list's parent must be positioned (the areas list's wrapper is now `relative`; the results list's already was). It is a sibling of the scroller, not inside it, so the fade's mask does not fade it. The areas list's side room went from 8px to 12px so the thumb sits clear of the row cards (measured: a visible gap).
  - **Options deleted (4.4).** The lab `/proto/scrollbars` and its line in the `/proto` index are removed now that an option is chosen; `2026-10-23-50` keeps the record of what a browser allows and what the four looked like.
  - **Documented:** `/patterns/map-list-card` (the list rule), `/principles/craft` (the scroll detail), the comments in `map-surface.ts`, `result-list.tsx`, `scroll-fade.tsx` and `area-layers.tsx`.
  - **Checked live (Playwright, with scrollbars drawn, BioData Super Admin; zero console errors):** Explore's results list: thumb invisible at rest, visible with the pointer over the list and for a moment after a scroll, gone 1.3s later; 6px wide, 3px from the card's edge (a 1px border and 2px); dragging 40px of thumb moved the list from 150 to 284; a click on a row's right edge still opened its summary. The areas list with six areas shows a thumb and no sideways scroll. Map visualise's list shows it. With touch emulated the thumb appears while the list scrolls and takes no pointer events. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the touched files and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
  - **Assumed, for the designer to correct:** (1) **Where:** the map cards' lists only, the three above. Every other scroller in the product (tables, a page's `main`, menus and popovers, the filter lists, the column-2 lists) keeps the browser's scrollbar and the `scrollbar-gutter` rule (CONTRACTS 4.2f). It could be extended to all of them with the same component; that is a larger change and a design decision. (2) **The fade stays** beside the thumb: the fade says there is more, the thumb says where and how much.
  - **Not covered, so it is not read as more than it is:** the record summary cards (`RecordPeekCard`, Map visualise's `ErrorSummaryCard`) have a short scroll area that hides the browser's bar with `MAP_SURFACE_SCROLL` and draws no thumb: they rarely scroll, and a thumb would need the same wrapper; say if they should. Not checked: a trackpad session scrolling slowly, Safari, a screen reader (the thumb is `aria-hidden`; the list is not announced as a scrollable region).
