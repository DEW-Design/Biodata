# 2026-10-13 - Post 6 Oct lab: the notifications popover, from Vercel

- **Oct 13 2026: Post 6 Oct lab: the notifications popover, from Vercel.** The designer, from a screenshot of Vercel's notifications popup ("much cleaner"), then, after a proposal of what else Vercel's shell offers: "let's only focus on the notifications popup. Why don't we steal Vercel's pattern?" In `/proto/post-6-oct` only. The pattern is Vercel's interaction (CONTRACTS 2.6: an idea, never its colour, spacing or type); the popover uses the product's tokens, Barlow and the DEW `Popover`, `Tabs`, `Button` and `FeaturedIcon`.

  **The pattern, as built (`notifications-popover.tsx`).** Inbox and Archive tabs with a count on Inbox. Each notification is one sentence with its key words in bold, a tinted icon at the left (warning for something waiting on you, error for flagged concepts, success or brand for a registered user's own tasks) and, at the right, an unread dot and when it happened, with a hairline between rows. Pointed at or focused, a row shows Done where the time was; Done moves it to Archive, where the same spot offers Move back to Inbox. The Inbox ends with a full-width "Mark all done"; an empty Inbox says "You're all caught up", an empty Archive "Nothing in the archive". A row opens the page it is about in this tab (the real page for one the lab does not hold) and counts as read; the bell's dot shows only while something is unread, and its label says how many. Read and Done are kept for the session by the shell, so they survive the bell moving (tab strip at step 1 and below, account row from step 2). The popover opens to the left from the tab strip and to the right from the rail.

  **What the rows are (`attention.tsx`, now one notification each).** A reviewer gets one row per request under review ("Request DLA-2026-00502 from Maya Dewitt is waiting for your decision") and one per nomination submitted and waiting for the panel, and one row for the flagged concepts open across the projects (too many for one each), counted by the same rule as each list's own banner. A Registered User gets the three tasks Home shows, in the lab's wording. A role with nothing waiting has an empty Inbox. A row shows a time only where the data has a date for the event (a request's and a nomination's last update); the others show none, and none is invented (0.3). Dates in the sample data are September, so they read as dates ("18 Sept"), not "5h".

  **Left out on purpose:** Vercel's Comments tab and settings gear (no comments feed and no notification settings page, so they would promise what is not there, 2.10), and its push-notification prompt (no push). The rail card from the first pass stays out.

  **Open for the designer (CONTRACTS 0.4):** whether a row should open the record itself instead of the filtered list (the real record page leaves the lab, since no record page is built here); whether "Done" should be a real state in the product (today it is the lab's, per session); whether a Comments tab belongs once flagged-concept comments become a feed; whether reviewers want a count on the rail rows as well as the bell.
