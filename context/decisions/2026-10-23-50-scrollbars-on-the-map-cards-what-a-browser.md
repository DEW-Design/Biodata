# 2026-10-23 - Scrollbars on the map cards: what a browser allows, and a lab of four treatments

- **Oct 23 2026: Scrollbars on the map cards: what a browser allows, and a lab of four treatments.** The designer, after the scrollbar under "Circle 3" (`2026-10-23-48`): "Is there a way we can hide/show scroll bar? and can we have only the scroll bar and not the container?" Read as: show the bar only when it is useful, and draw the thumb without its track (the "container"). Nothing in the product was changed; this is the answer and a lab (`/proto/scrollbars`, in the `/proto` index) so the options can be scrolled with a real mouse before one is chosen (0.10, 4.4).
  - **What a browser allows (Chromium 153 with scrollbars drawn; my earlier passes ran with them hidden, which is how the "Circle 3" bar was missed):**
    - Hidden: `scrollbar-width: none`, what `MAP_SURFACE_SCROLL` does today. Lane taken from the content: 0px.
    - Classic: a 15px lane with a visible track and a thumb.
    - Thin: `scrollbar-width: thin`, 11px, still with a lighter track.
    - **The bar without its track:** `scrollbar-width: thin; scrollbar-color: <thumb> transparent`. Yes, the track goes. The browser still reserves its 11px lane, so the content is 11px narrower than the card (measured), and a card with a lane and no visible track looks lopsided, which is the reason the card hides the bar today (`MAP_SURFACE_SCROLL`'s note).
    - **Shown only when pointed at:** `scrollbar-color: transparent transparent`, then the thumb colour on `:hover` and `:focus-visible`. Yes, it works. The lane is reserved at rest too (11px, empty), so the content is narrower with nothing in the space.
    - **No lane at all** cannot be done with CSS: a classic bar cannot be made to float (`overflow: overlay` was removed), and a Mac set to "Show scroll bars: Always" gets classic bars whatever a page asks. The only way to take no space on every setting is to hide the browser's bar and draw a thumb over the content.
  - **The lab:** the same three lists (14 rows, 6 rows, 3 rows that fit) in the card's real width and with the real result rows, with A hidden (today), B the bar only (thin, no track, always), C the bar only on hover, D **a drawn thumb** (`DrawnScroll`, in the lab file: 6px, no track, over the content's right edge, shown while the list scrolls and for 0.9s after, while the pointer is over it and while it is dragged; the list scrolls by wheel, touch and keyboard as before). The last line under each card says the lane it takes. Checked in a browser with scrollbars drawn: A 0px; B 11px; C 11px; D 0px; D's thumb is invisible at rest, visible with the pointer over the list and while scrolling, gone 1.3s after the last scroll, drags the list (60px of thumb moved the list from 200 to 319), is not drawn when everything fits, and PageDown scrolls the focused list; zero console errors. `tsc`, `eslint` and `check:contracts` clean.
  - **What D costs, so the choice is informed:** it is a small component to own (the thumb is not a native control: no click on a track to page, no OS styling, and a screen reader is given nothing, since the list itself stays the focusable, scrollable region); it would move from the lab to `app/pages/_shared` and be used by `MapResultList`, `ScrollFade` (the list axis) and the areas list. B and C cost nothing to build but keep an 11px lane.
  - **Open, for the designer:** which of A to D, and where it applies: the map cards' lists only (the results list, the areas list, Map visualise's list), or every scrolling list and table in the product; and whether the fade at the foot stays alongside a bar (today the fade is what says there is more). The contracts say nothing about the scrollbar of a list today except the table's gutter (4.2f) and the map card's hidden bar (`MAP_SURFACE_SCROLL`), so a choice would become a clause.
