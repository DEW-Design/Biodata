# 2026-10-06 - Pages panel: Recent is a card of its own, All pages scrolls under its label

- **Oct 6 2026: Recent and All pages were two headings in one scrolling list (`2026-10-06-01-*`), so scrolling carried Recent away and the two blended. From the designer: "Could it be more nicely visually separated. Recents separate from all pages."**
  - **Recent is a card.** A bordered white card (`ring-primary`, gray-300, rounded) inside the panel, with its own small "Recent" label and the four pages in the same tree styling. It does not scroll. A recent page deeper than its area (not a screen named like its area) carries its area in small muted text at the right, so "Nomination" is told from "Nominations". The card's border is `ring-primary` because `ring-secondary` (gray-200) was barely visible on the white panel; the card stays white because the tree's row hover is gray-50, the same as `bg-secondary`, and a tinted card would hide the hover.
  - **All pages is the other region.** Its label sits fixed above the scrolling tree, so the tree scrolls under its heading. While searching, Recent goes and the label reads "Results" with the number found; otherwise the page count stays only in the footer (it was in both and was removed from the heading, less is more).
  - **One height.** The body is `h-[min(56vh,480px)]` whether or not there is a Recent card, so the panel does not change size between a fresh browser and a used one (the tree takes what Recent leaves).
  - **Verified live (Playwright, 1708x1024, Registered User, on DLA, zero console errors):** Recent (Home, Projects, Nominations, Reports) as a card, All pages below with its own label, the current row tinted and in view inside the scrolling region; searching removes the card and leaves one tree; the docs page still renders. `tsc`, eslint on `app/_prototype-tools` and `check:contracts` pass.
  - **Not done:** Emil typography pass on the labels, a keyboard-only pass across the two trees (Down from the box lands in Recent first, then the tree is reached by Tab), narrow windows.
