- **Sept 29 2026: `/proto/tools` Status bar is dark, its motion is cut down, and it hides with an X, per page feedback ("see the status bar in dark mode... it just blends in to the UI"; "the switcher animation is too jarring... reduce the amount of animation"; the hide chevron "Let this be X instead").**
  - **Dark:** `bg-gray-950` (the Figma-confirmed near-black the dev tools already use) with a `white/10` ring and white text at three weights (value white, label white/60, icons white/50). The menus are dark to match. A forced override reads in `error-300` (lighter red, legible on black) instead of `error-primary`.
  - **Less motion:** changing a value is instant, with no odometer roll and no width animation (traced: the surface moves in one frame). A tool that appears fades in over 150ms. Only hiding and showing animate: the surface eases its width over 200ms ease-out, no bounce, right edge fixed, content cross-fading. The drag lift and blur are gone, and menus are a plain 150ms fade (this applies to the Dock and Launcher menus too, since they share the menu). Reduced motion still turns everything off.
  - **Hide is an X (`XClose`)**; the collapsed tab keeps an up chevron to say it opens, and the chips' chevrons point up because their menus open upward.
  - **Bugs found and fixed while verifying:** the hide/show ease was being cancelled by the resize observer's first report, by React attaching the measuring ref twice in development, and by the fading-out content reporting its own width; the width now follows only the showing content and never re-applies a size it is already heading to. The previous round's `outline-none` fix had also hidden the keyboard focus ring in all three variants; the ring is back for keyboard focus only (`data-focus-visible:outline-solid`), and a mouse pick still leaves none.
  - **Verified live at 1708x1024:** every screen preset lists the right tools, a role change moves the bar in one frame, hide and show ease across about 12 frames, keyboard ring visible, zero console errors; `tsc` and `eslint --max-warnings=0` clean.
