# 2026-10-23 - Map list card: one pattern for Explore and Map visualise, documented in the design system

- **Oct 23 2026: Map list card: one pattern for Explore and Map visualise, documented in the design system.** The designer, with a screenshot of the finished Map visualise card: "can this pattern be documented in the design system? Let's also make sure the updates we've made to craft it to this level is applied to the species tab as well. Let's bring this polish across." Explore's results card (the Species tab and its five siblings, which share one list) had none of the session's work on Map visualise's list: a classic scrollbar, "Show N more" as a link at the end of the rows, no fade, tabs that left the old scroll position, and a summary card with the old grid of facts.
  - **Built as shared parts, so the two cards cannot drift (`app/pages/_shared/map-search/`):**
    - **`MapResultList` (`result-list.tsx`):** the list on a floating card, with everything this session settled: it fades out at its foot while there is more below (`ScrollFade axis="y"`, 120px, about a row and a half, capped at 45 per cent of a short list) and is faded clear under the button; no scrollbar and no scroll chaining (`MAP_SURFACE_SCROLL`); "Show N more" as a secondary Button pinned over the foot, 16px up, on `shadow-2xl`, outside the scroller; the list padded 68px at its foot while the button is there; a press scrolls the new rows into view (a heading that starts them first) and a keyboard press moves focus to the first. The logic that lived in `dataset-error-map.tsx` (the reveal effect, the constants, the pinned block) moved into it; Map visualise now uses it with the same numbers (re-measured: the mask, the 68px pad, the button 16px up, 16px between the last row and the button at the end, the keyboard focus on row 25, all as before).
    - **`SummaryFacts` and `SummaryCloseButton` (`summary-card-parts.tsx`):** the facts as rows with lines, and the close control in the top-right corner (solid chip over a photo, bare over the plain header, its own hit area). Both summary cards use them.
  - **Applied to Explore (`observations-search.tsx`, `record-peek-card.tsx`):** the results list is a `MapResultList` of the same `ResultCard`s (the rows themselves are unchanged: photo or icon, common name, scientific name in italics, provenance); the list is reset to the top when a tab changes (it kept the last tab's scroll position); the record peek card, which a species row opens, shows its facts as rows with lines, as Map visualise's summary does (a peek of an occurrence is 344px tall with its photo; it is height-bounded and scrolls its details as a last resort, as before). This also closes the open item in `2026-10-23-31-*` (Explore's scrollers still drew a classic scrollbar) and in `-39` (the peek card still had the grid).
  - **The pattern is documented at `/patterns/map-list-card`** (`app/(docs)/patterns/map-list-card/page.tsx`; in `lib/nav.ts` Patterns and the README table, 5.3): anatomy (header, tabs strip, list, key, summary card, zoom), a live list longer than a page with the real `MapResultList`, `ResultCard`s and tabs (press Show 25 more: it scrolls and, from the keyboard, moves focus), the summary card's rows, the key, and the rules, each pointing at the clause behind it (2.8 panels never overlap, 2.14 the key, 2.15 a view from a table, 2.16 the hit area, `MAP_SURFACE_EDGE`). The sample rows are the Explore dataset's real species repeated to make a long list, and the page says so. No Figma file: it was designed in the browser.
  - **Checked live (Playwright, scripts outside the repo; BioData Super Admin):** Explore with a keyword that matches 60 records: every tab's list has the mask while it overflows and none at its end, no scrollbar (`offsetWidth - clientWidth` 0), `overscroll-behavior-y: contain`, the tab change returns the list to scrollTop 0, the peek card of a row has three rows with 1px rules, values right-aligned; in a screenshot the last row of Occurrences ("Southern Bell Frog") is half gone at the foot. The docs page: HTTP 200, 25 rows then "Show 25 more", a keyboard press shows 25 more with focus on row 25 and "Show 6 more", the second press shows the last 6 and the button is gone, the mask goes at the end, no scrollbar; zero console errors. Map visualise re-run after moving onto the shared list: list, foot spacing, hit-area presses (16 of 16), the species order against the report, the lined summary: all as before. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
