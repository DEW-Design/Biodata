# 2026-10-23 - Touch targets are built into the components: one hit-area utility, applied across the webapp

- **Oct 23 2026: Touch targets are built into the components: one hit-area utility, applied across the webapp.** The designer: "make sure the touch target bits are applied on a component level, then applied all across the webapp." `2026-10-23-25-*` gave Map visualise its touch targets one control at a time, with a `::before` written at each call site (a `TAB_TARGET` constant on every tab, `before:-inset-y-1` on Show more, offsets on the back link and Hide list). Those are now the components' job, and the rest of the webapp gets them for the same reason: the controls are the same components.
  - **The mechanism (`app/globals.css`):** a `hit-area` utility. A control that draws smaller than `--hit-min` gets an invisible box of that size, centred on it, as an `::after` of the control (so a press on it is a press on the control): `--hit-min` is **40px with a mouse and 44px where the pointer is coarse** (`@media (pointer: coarse)`), the rule from `/emil-ui-polish` (44 for touch, at least 40 on desktop). It is `max(100%, var(--hit-min))` on each axis, so a control already that big is untouched, and it never changes how a control looks or where it sits. A control with no position of its own is made `relative` by a rule in the **base layer** (an unlayered rule would have beaten `absolute` and moved every absolutely placed control; found when a password field lost 16px, and fixed). Two kinds of override for controls that touch: `[--hit-min:32px]` on a group caps its controls' targets, and `after:content-none` or a `data-no-hit-area` ancestor removes them. A control with a border centres on its padding box, so it can offset by `--hit-dx`/`--hit-dy` (the underline tab's 2px bottom border is `[--hit-dy:1px]`: without it the tab's target was 5px above and 3px below).
  - **Built into the design system's components (so every screen that uses them has it):** `Button` (every size and colour, and the link-style ones; off when it is joined to an input in an `InputGroup`), `ButtonUtility`, `CloseButton`, `Tab` (horizontal; off for the vertical tabs stacked in column 2), the input's clear and show-password buttons (which carried their own hand-written `before:-inset-2.5`, now the shared one), the table's page-number buttons, `Checkbox`, `RadioButton`, `Toggle` (the whole labelled control), the dropdown's "..." button, `TooltipTrigger`, and the date picker's calendar, month and Today buttons. Beside them, the shared shell: the header's breadcrumb (every crumb a shell draws for itself, from `AppHeader`, once), `BreadcrumbSwitcher`, `ProfileMenu`, `RecordBackLink`, the report star and menu, the landing pages' navigation links.
  - **Removed from the call sites:** Map visualise's `TAB_TARGET`, Show more's, the back link's and Hide list's `before:` offsets, and the summary close button's `before:absolute before:-inset-1.5` (now `hit-area`). The tabs' room in their strip is `ScrollFade`'s own (below).
  - **Where a scroller would have taken the extension as scroll, it has room instead.** A tab's target reaches up to 6px beyond it, and a container that scrolls sideways scrolls or clips that. `ScrollFade` (the strip under Explore's results and Map visualise's categories) now has 6px of padding above and below, taken back by an equal margin, so it takes the room it did (measured: 13px from the list's top to the tabs and 8px from the tabs to the first row, as before; the Explore strip 32px tall, no vertical scroll, was 4px of phantom scroll). Four other strips that scroll sideways (the Reports landing's and the taxonomy reports' category tabs, and the species and rank record tabs) got the same `pb-1.5 -mb-1.5`. A phantom-scroll check over 80 screens, with and without the extension, finds none.
  - **Measured, the way a person would meet it (Playwright, scripts outside the repo; 80 screens from the screen index, a mouse and a touch-emulated context where `matchMedia("(pointer: coarse)")` is true):** of 2,315 controls (14 inline text links not counted), 1,340 reached 40px before and **1,854 reach it with a mouse now**; 1,820 reach 44px with touch. By pressing: on Map visualise a tab, Show more, Hide list, Show list and the back link each take a press just inside their extension (1 to 11px, by control and pointer) and not one just outside it, with the mouse and with touch (16 of 16); the header's crumbs, 20px tall, are 40 and 44. A check for a target overlapping another control finds none that is real (it lists the dev feedback overlay, a button partly under a sticky footer, and the show-password button over the field it sits in, by design). Zero console errors; `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on every touched file (three old findings in the files touched, an empty interface twice and an unused discard, are fixed with no change in behaviour) and `npm run check:contracts` clean. Computed positions of every hit-area control checked against its own classes: one differs by design (the alert's close button is `relative` from the md breakpoint, as `md:static` before). Screenshots of Login, Explore and the dashboard look as they did.
  - **What was left as drawn, and why (each with a count of how many of the 2,315):** text fields and selects, about 125 (36 or 37px tall, a typing target with its label; extending them would take 2 to 4px from the space between fields, and they are not the small targets); the global search's field, 79 (the real input sits inside a taller control); rows of a list or menu stacked edge to edge (column 2's tabs and action rows, the on-this-page anchors, the progress steps and the accordions), about 140 (an extension would cover the row beside it; they are as tall as their rows); the remove icon on a tag or badge (it sits on the chip's own label, which the extension would take over); the accordion and tree chevrons (stacked rows); the taxonomy page's List and Table switch (a segmented control that is its own group). Links and buttons that a page draws by hand and are not these components (a few dozen: the observation page's own "Back to" and crumbs, a favourites star, the draft pages) were not touched except the star, the landing links and the crumbs above; the right fix for them is to use the component, which is for the designer to decide (below).
  - **A trade-off to know:** the default is on for every Button, which is the designer's instruction ("applied all across the webapp"), but CONTRACTS 1.6 says an addition to a component is opt-in with a default that leaves every caller unchanged. It leaves every caller's look and layout unchanged and only enlarges what a press hits, so I took the instruction over the letter, and name it here rather than in the contract. Where two small controls sit closer than twice the extension, the group caps it (the report card's star and menu are `[--hit-min:32px]`); a new screen that puts such controls together has to do the same, which is `REVIEW`, so it can come back.
  - **Not verified:** a real touch device (a touch-emulated Chromium was used, where `pointer: coarse` is true), Safari and Firefox (the extension is an ordinary `::after`), a screen reader (nothing changes in the tree), and the dozens of hand-built controls above. The extension is clipped, as any child is, by an ancestor with `overflow: hidden` (a link that truncates keeps only its box, for one); the audit shows those as the few 20px crumbs and the "View resources" link.
  - **Open, with a recommendation:** (1) make it a contract, "a control's hit area is built into its component", `REVIEW` with this decision's measurement as its check, plus one `AUTO` check that a Button, ButtonUtility, CloseButton or Tab file keeps `hit-area` (and one that flags a `before:-inset` or `after:-inset` written to enlarge a target outside `components/`); recommend yes, say so and it is added. (2) The hand-built lookalikes (the observation page's back link and crumbs, the small icon buttons and links that are not `ButtonUtility` or `RecordBackLink`) should become those components, which then gives them the hit area with no more code; that is a visible change to each page, so it waits. (3) Whether the text fields should be 40px tall on touch (a design system change to `Input`, not a hit area). Not committed.
