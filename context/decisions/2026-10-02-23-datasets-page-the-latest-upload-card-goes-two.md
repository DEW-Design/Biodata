# 2026-10-02 - Datasets page: the latest-upload card goes two-column and the page scrolls, so the table is not squeezed

- **Oct 2 2026: Datasets page: the latest-upload card goes two-column and the page scrolls, so the table is not squeezed.** Annotated screenshot of the project's Datasets page (BD-5039, a failed upload of 27,685 rows): "This layout is messed up, please fix."
  - **Cause, two faults:** (1) the "Latest upload" card stacked the progress bar, the three stages and the reasons and actions in one column no wider than 42rem, so it ran about 680px tall with its right half empty; (2) the content area under the page header did not scroll, so the table below it (a 192px floor and its pagination) was clipped: one row half cut off at the top, the pager running off the bottom.
  - **Fix 1:** `IngestionDetail` has an opt-in `split` prop (default off, so the chip's popover and the dataset page are unchanged). The Datasets page turns it on: the run (progress and the stages) on the left at 26rem, a rule, and what went wrong with its actions (Upload a corrected file, View in Reports) on the right. The card is 328px tall (was about 680px) and uses the width. It stays stacked below the `lg` breakpoint, and a clean in-progress upload (no reasons yet) is unchanged.
  - **Fix 2:** the area under the header scrolls (`overflow-y-auto [scrollbar-gutter:stable]`, 4.2f) and the table's floor is 24rem (header, five rows and the pager), so a tall card and a window that is short scroll the content instead of crushing the table. The header and Back to project stay put.
  - **Measured with the same failed upload seeded into the browser:** card 328px at all three sizes; table 384px high with all its rows and its pager reachable by scrolling at 1280 x 720 and 1440 x 900, and in view without scrolling at 1708 x 1024; zero console errors. A partial outcome (needs attention) lays out the same way.
