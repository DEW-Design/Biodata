- **Sept 29 2026: `/proto/tools` Status bar refined for approval (not in production), per direct feedback ("I love status bar... labels true to what's being shown, don't make me think... get /emil-animations sorted... a nice delight moment").** Only the Status bar variant changed (now `app/proto/tools/status-bar.tsx`); the Dock and Launcher are kept until the designer decides whether to delete them (CONTRACTS 4.4, 0.4).
  - **Rule, built in:** the bar lists only the tools the page passes it; a page with nothing to preview shows only the role. The lab's "pretend this is" select plays the per-screen table (Explore, Add Project, Adelaide Hills with and without a dataset, another project with a dataset, and the role-only screens), and each gives exactly its tools, checked live.
  - **Labels read as a phrase of what is on screen:** "Viewing as Registered User", "Layout Option 2 of 2", "Upload result Succeeds". Role names are the ones User Management uses (BioData Admin, BioData User, Privileged Admin, Privileged User, Registered User), plus "Public user, Signed out". Menus open with the question they answer ("View the app as", "Layout to show", "How the upload ends") and give one line under each option saying what you will see ("Nothing is added", "Search, then a results page"). A long outcome is shortened in the bar ("Fails: our side") with its full name in the menu. The collapsed tab spells out the role and any forced override ("Upload result Fails: our side" in red) instead of the earlier bare red dot, which read as if the role had a problem.
  - **Motion (emil-animations):** the bar is one surface that changes shape. Collapse and expand, and a tool arriving or leaving, animate the surface's measured width with its right edge fixed (a spring, about 400ms, bounce 0.12); new content is revealed by the edge sweeping open and existing tools slide with position-only transforms. Width is animated on purpose: the bar is `position: fixed`, and the first scale-based version made a newly added tool ride over the role for about 200ms (traced frame by frame). A changed value rolls like an odometer in the direction of its place in the list, with a light blur; dragging lifts the bar to 1.02 with a deeper shadow; menus scale from their trigger with a small nudge (150ms in, 100ms out). No animation on first paint or keyboard focus moves; reduced motion turns all of it off (checked). Press feedback is `scale(0.96)`.
  - **Fixed on the way (all three variants):** a focus ring stayed on a button after a mouse pick (the browser's own outline when focus returns from the menu); rings are now keyboard-only.
  - **Verified live (Playwright, 1600x1000):** every screen preset, value roll, forced override in red, collapse and expand, arrow keys along the bar, Enter opens a menu and Escape returns focus, Geist text, reduced motion instant, zero console errors. `tsc` and `eslint --max-warnings=0` clean. Not committed; waits for the designer's approval before it replaces `RoleSwitcher`, `LayoutOptionSwitcher`, `FloatingMenuFab` and the ingestion outcome button.
