# 2026-10-16 - Reports: one mock Example record in the empty states

- **Oct 16 2026: one record in the empty states.** The designer: "In the empty states, can we just add one record. The one record could just be mock data." Read as the report tabs that showed "No ... yet": the Flora Taxonomy Report's Species attributes, Rename, Split, Append and Species synonym tabs, and the Project Audit Log Report ("No changes yet"). The other reports already have rows.

  **How.** `DataReport` gained an opt-in `example` prop. While a report has no row of its own, the table shows that one record in place of the empty state, with an **Example record** badge on the toolbar beside the Columns button; as soon as a real row exists the example is gone. It is never stored, and nothing in Taxonomy Management or the project store shows it. The search and the filters act on it like any row: searching it away shows the usual "No renames match" state with its way back. A report that passes no `example` keeps its empty state (4.2e).
  - **The six records** (`taxonomy-example-rows.ts`, and `EXAMPLE` in `project-audit-log-report.tsx`), each made of the placeholder people (0.3) and made-up values: Species attributes (Eucalyptus leucoxylon, current and official, South Australia, Southern Lofty, Life form, Growth form, assessed 4 Aug 2026); Rename (Eucalyptus leucoxylon renamed to subsp. pruinosa by Lana Steiner, 14 Oct 2026); Split (Xanthorrhoea semiplana split, one of the species it made, subsp. semiplana, by Phoenix Baker); Append (Acacia pycnantha, Fabaceae, by Olivia Wyatt); Species synonym (Eucalyptus leucoxylon var. exemplaris, published, matches CC); Project Audit Log (a CREATE of BD-5039 Adelaide Hills Bushland Survey by Olivia Wyatt, 4 Aug 2026). The NSX codes P04412 and P09999 are made up.
  - **The hero** counts and dates read the rows in view, so with the example on screen it counts it (the Rename tab's Latest record reads 14 Oct 2026); an export of an empty report includes the example row, marked only by its content.
