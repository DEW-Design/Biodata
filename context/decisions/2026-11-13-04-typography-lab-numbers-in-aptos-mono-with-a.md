# 2026-11-13 - Typography lab: numbers in Aptos Mono with a slashed zero

- **Nov 13 2026: Typography lab: numbers in Aptos Mono with a slashed zero.** The designer, after `2026-11-13-03-*`: "Aptos mono for numbers, btw. Slashed zero setting." Added to the lab's "after" only (`app/proto/typography/page.tsx`, views B and C); nothing outside `app/proto` changed.
  - **How:** a font-face named "Aptos Digits" whose `unicode-range` is the digits only (U+0030-0039), over the installed Aptos Mono (`local()`: Regular for weights up to 599, Bold from 600), put first in the stack for the body (`--font-barlow`) and for headings (`--type-title`). So every digit in a sentence, an ID, a date, a phone number or a count takes it, on any screen, and the rest of the line stays Aptos (or Barlow in a heading), with no component edited. The slashed zero is the font's own `zero` feature, `font-feature-settings: "zero" 1`, set once on the scope and inherited. Checked in a browser: the feature changes the glyph in both weights, and Aptos Mono's default zero is already slashed (a diagonal), the feature gives its straight slash.
  - **Decided by the lab, not by the designer (0.4):** numbers in headings are mono too (a number in a title is a number); easy to flip. The comma, point, slash, colon and brackets inside a number stay Aptos, because a range over those characters would also turn them mono in ordinary sentences, so "(08) 8388 4188" is mono digits with Aptos brackets and space. A letter-based identifier ("BD") stays Aptos; only its digits change.
  - **Costs, measured (Chromium, 1600px, the specimen's own strings):** Aptos Mono digits are 0.6em wide, so numbers are wider than Barlow's: BD-5039 52.5 to 56.5px (+7.7 per cent), "2 Nov 2025" 66.0 to 71.5 (+8.4), "(08) 8388 4188" 86.7 to 97.9 (+12.9), "28" 14.8 to 16.8; the words beside them are +1.7 per cent. Aptos Mono has Regular and Bold only (no Medium, no SemiBold): digits in 400 and 500 text draw Regular, in 600 text (the tab counts, badges, card titles) draw Bold, heavier than the Aptos SemiBold next to them. The lab says both on the page and warns on a machine without Aptos Mono (numbers fall back to Aptos).
  - **Verified:** the stack and the `zero` setting are computed on the body, labels and headings in the frame and in the specimen; before is unchanged (Barlow, `normal`); real project page: Project ID, start date, tab counts and the contact number are mono with slashed zeros; zero console errors; `tsc`, `eslint` and `check:contracts` pass. Not measured: the Projects list and Add project screens with the wider numbers (columns, date fields and tab counts could take them differently), a table's right-aligned figures, contrast, Windows.
  - **Open (0.4):** adopting it is the same decision as the pairing (a token change and a clause change, and a licensed way to serve Aptos and Aptos Mono); and the wider numbers need a look in a fixed table column (4.2f) and the date field. Not committed.
