# 2026-10-23 - Explore add-area forms: the choice and the radius on one line, a tinted step, no clipped rows

- **Oct 23 2026: Explore add-area forms: the choice and the radius on one line, a tinted step, no clipped rows.** The designer, with a screenshot of "Choose national parks" open (a title and Done, a full-width "3 selected" field, a "Search radius (km)" field below it, then the search field, results heading, chips, tabs and list): "we'll need to polish this experience. Choose and radius can be on one line, also, the hierarchy now looks very unintuitive. Can we please fix?" (refined with `/refine-prompt`: the table and the prompt were shown with the reply).
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 2 One thing first (the form is one step and it had no order), 3 One system (the three forms take one pattern), 1 Say less (one line for a choice and its radius, not two stacked fields). They agree here; none pulls against another.
  - **The fault, measured:** the form was a stack of full-width fields at the same size, weight and colour as the search field under it, with nothing between them but 12px, so the eye had no first place and the search field read as the form's next field. "Search radius (km)" was a second full-width row that only qualified the choice above it.
  - **The change (all three forms, `observations-search.tsx`):**
    1. **The choice and its radius are one row.** Parks: the multiselect grows, the radius is a fixed 7rem (`w-28`) beside it, 12px apart. Coordinates: latitude, longitude and radius in one three-column row (`1fr 1fr 7rem`), then Add point. Shapefile: the file field grows, the radius is beside it, the hint under the row. "Search radius (km)" is "Radius (km)" now that it sits beside the thing it measures.
    2. **Small fields (36px, 14px type),** the size of the buttons and the search field around them, with the label above each, persistent (a placeholder is not a label).
    3. **The step is a region of its own:** a tinted strip (`bg-secondary`) between two rules, the panel title (14/600) over it, so the search field below reads as the card's search and not as the form's last field. Measured: title 14px/600, labels 14px/500, fields 36px, the search field 14px/400, all from the design system's components, no new type layer (2.9).
  - **A bug found on the way, and fixed:** the panel's wrapper was `max-h-[50%]`, a percentage of a card that is only as tall as its content, so it resolved to half the card and cut the form: the coordinates panel was 168px tall in a 148px box (Add point cut in half and scrolling inside itself) and the shapefile hint was cut at its first line. The cap is now `max-h-[50vh]`, a share of the window, which does not depend on the card. Measured at 1686x900, 1686x560 and 720x800: panel height equals its box height, no scroll, the last row 13px inside the panel (12px padding and the rule) for all three forms. The sibling grep found no other `max-h-[50%]` in `app/pages/observations` or `map-search`.
  - **Checked live (Playwright, Super Admin; zero console errors):** each form opened and screenshot at 2x; two parks chosen gave two chips; a coordinate entered gave a point chip; Cancel and Done close each form. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the touched file and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
  - **Not checked:** touch, Safari, a screen reader through the new row (the labels are the fields' own).
  - **Open, for the designer:** whether the teal "Add area" at the header should stay where it is while a form is open (it is still pressable and opens another form, which replaces this one); whether the tint should go (it is the one place the card has a second surface colour); and whether "Done" on the parks form (the only form that has no Add button, since each park chosen is added at once) should read differently from "Cancel" on the other two, as it does now.
