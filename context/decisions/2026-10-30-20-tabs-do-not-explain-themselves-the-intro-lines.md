# 2026-10-30 - Tabs do not explain themselves: the intro lines on Species, Artefacts and Datasets are gone

- **Oct 30 2026: the one-line introductions under the project page's Species, Artefacts and Datasets tab bars are removed.** The designer, with a screenshot of the Species tab's line ("Every species this project has recorded, from its occurrences in Survey records. "Go to record" opens the occurrence."): "all these text - read as AI generated, and looks distasteful." The three lines were the same kind of sentence: they say what the tab is (its name already does) and how a control works (the control is labelled). The Records tab never had one, and reads better for it.
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 1 Say less (nothing on screen without a purpose, 2.12), 9 Craft shows. They agree.
  - **What changed.** The line is gone from `species-view.tsx`, `artefacts-view.tsx` and `datasets-view.tsx`, in the empty state as well as the full tab. Each tab now starts with its own content, 24px under the tab bar as before. Nothing was added in its place. The words that carried a rule or a state (the empty states, "No ... match") were not intro lines and stay.
  - **Looked for others like it.** Every tab panel in `app/pages` that opens with a paragraph was found (a search for a `TabPanel` whose first child is a paragraph): the only others are the DSA's and the user's "Last updated" date, which is data, and the location picker's "Draw a polygon or circle on the map to define this area", an instruction for the one control under it. None was changed.
  - **A line in the contracts, so it is not found by a person again (0.8).** CONTRACTS 2.12 now says a tab, a heading or a field does not carry a sentence that says what it is; a sentence there is for something the person could not otherwise know. It is a judgement of wording, so the enforcement is `REVIEW`, with the origin.
  - **Checked.** `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the touched folder and `npm run check:contracts` pass. Live (Playwright, Adelaide Hills as BioData Admin, zero console errors): Species, Datasets and Artefacts have no intro paragraph and start 24px under the tab bar; Species shows its tiles, search and cards straight away; the empty states of a new project are the card alone.
  - **Not changed, and open for the designer (0.4).** Other copy of the same kind that is still on screen, for you to say which is distasteful: the Overview's section lines ("Where, how and under what permits the data was collected.", "What is held back from public release, and why."); the form subtitles under a step title (Add Project, the upload form), which the form pattern asks for (4.1); and some of what was written this week for the new Add Project Datasets step and the empty states ("Optional. Add one or more files now, or skip this step and add datasets later from the project's Upload menu.", "What the file itself states about the project's data.", the privacy note under the dataset's confirmation, and the one-line reasons in the empty states). These were written to explain, which is the same fault; each can be cut to a title and a button.
