# 2026-10-13 - Post 6 Oct lab: views are tabs you can see, not a dropdown

- **Oct 13 2026: Post 6 Oct lab: views are tabs you can see, not a dropdown.** The designer, from a screenshot of the bar's view menu open on a project (Overview, Project records, Species, Artefacts and attachments, Edit details): "this feels like an anti-pattern, no? Can you see Linear for patterns on how to fix this? This is how we're currently switching between tabs, which doesn't quite make sense." In `/proto/post-6-oct` only. Agreed: the views of one thing are peers, and the way between them was an icon-only pill that said nothing of where you were and hid every other view behind a click and a menu.

  **Linear's answer (Mobbin, 13 Oct 2026; the same screens as the proposal of 12 Oct):** the views of a page are tabs you can see, at the top of the page, left aligned, one click each, the current one a soft grey pill. A project is "Overview, Updates, Issues" under its title and property line; a list is "All issues, Active, Backlog" above its rows, with the filter and display controls as icons at the right. The bar holds where you are and the page's actions, not its views.

  **Done.** (1) A project's views are tabs under its title at every step: Overview, Project records (count), Species (count), Artefacts and attachments (count), and Edit details for a role that can edit (`project-tabs.tsx`; the step 5 page uses the same). (2) A section's views, which are what column 2 listed (All requests and My requests, Users and Roles and Permissions, All species and Flora and Fauna, a notification category, Home's two dashboards), are tabs above the list (`ViewTabs`, `panel-controls.tsx`; drawn by `SectionContent`). A section with one view, or none, draws none (a one-option switcher is dishonest). (3) The view dropdown and its code are gone from the bar, which now holds the breadcrumb, the records tree and the "..." menu only. The tabs are the DEW tab list in its grey button type, not a lookalike.

  **Keyboard:** the tabs are in the tab order as one stop; the arrow keys move between them and Enter or Space selects (the DEW tab list's manual activation, which suits a view that loads a whole page).

  **Seen, not changed:** the nested rail still lists the same All and My choices as the section tabs now show, so a section's scopes appear in two places (the rail and the tabs); one could go. The tabs add a row above each list (about 44px); at the steps that remove headers this is the row that carries the list's title in Linear.

  **Open for the designer (CONTRACTS 0.4):** whether the rail's children should go now that the tabs show the same choices (they would then open a section straight at its first view only); whether a list's tabs should share a row with its search and the new-item action, as Linear's do, instead of sitting above them.

  Verified: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint app/proto` and `npm run check:contracts` clean. Live pass in headless Chrome at 1440 by 1024: at step 0 and step 5 a project shows the five tabs with counts, no dropdown in the bar, a click on Species selects it; as BioData Super Admin the DLA list shows All requests and My requests and My requests selects; as a Registered User, whose DLA has one view, there are no tabs; Projects at step 4 shows All projects and My projects. A sweep of every role at every step (every section, Projects, a project and, at step 5, its tabs): zero console errors. Not run: Enter and Space on a focused tab by script (the DEW tab list's standard behaviour, observed to move focus with the arrows); a screen reader pass. Not committed.
