# 2026-10-23 - One scrollbar for every scroller in the product: the drawn thumb, built once

- **Oct 23 2026: The scrollbar of the Explore lists (option D, a drawn thumb) is now the scrollbar of the whole product.** The designer: "the scroll bar logic we added on the explore screens, let's make that consistent across the webapp." `2026-10-23-53` had left exactly this open: "It could be extended to all of them with the same component; that is a larger change and a design decision." This is that decision, so it is a new clause, CONTRACTS 2.17.
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 3 One system (one scrollbar, built once, not at about 120 scrollers), 9 Craft shows (a lane that takes no width, so nothing moves), 8 Reach everyone (wheel, touch and keyboard unchanged; a thumb you can take hold of with a mouse). They agree. The one that pulls: 8 against a custom control (no click on a track to page); see Open.
  - **What was there:** the drawn thumb lived inside `ScrollFade` (`axis="y"`), so only three lists had it. A survey found about 120 other scrolling containers in product code: 35 page `main`s with `scrollbar-gutter: stable`, about 20 column-2 asides, about 25 list and record wrappers, the table body, five menu and select popovers, modals and side panels, and strips. They showed the browser's bar, 15px wide where a classic one is drawn.
  - **The mechanism, and why it is not a wrapper:** wrapping each scroller in a component means editing about 120 places and every new scroller has to remember. Instead:
    1. **`app/globals.css`:** one rule turns the browser's bar off everywhere (`* { scrollbar-width: none }`, `*::-webkit-scrollbar { display: none }`).
    2. **`<DrawnScrollbars />`** (`app/_scrollbars/drawn-scrollbars.tsx`, mounted once in `app/layout.tsx`) listens for scroll (captured) and for the pointer, finds the nearest element around it that scrolls, and draws its thumb in one fixed layer above everything (`SCROLLBAR_Z_INDEX`, `lib/layers.ts`, above react-aria's 100000 so a menu's own thumb is not under it). A new scroller needs `overflow-y-auto` and a height, nothing else.
    3. **The look is D, unchanged:** 6px, no track, 2px in from the edge, 8px short of each end, gray-400 (gray-500 held or pointed at); shown while it scrolls and 0.9s after, while the pointer is over the scroller or the thumb, and while dragged; the drag moves the scroller by the ratio of the lengths; indicator only on a touch screen. Both axes: a wide table has its thumb along its foot.
    4. **Where it is not drawn:** a box under 64px either way (a tab strip, a row of chips; their fade, `ScrollFade`, is their cue); anything marked `data-no-drawn-scrollbar` (the photo carousel and the lightbox's thumbnails); and any part of a thumb that has something else over it (a modal, a menu, the Prototype tools bar), sampled at both ends and the middle, so a long thumb under the bar is hidden and not drawn across it.
    5. **`ScrollFade` keeps only the fade.** Its thumb, its states and the rule that the list's parent must be positioned are gone (the thumb is in its own layer, so the mask does not fade it and nothing clips it). `MAP_SURFACE_SCROLL` is `overscroll-contain` only. The hand-made `scrollbar-width` rules in the carousel and the lightbox are gone.
  - **Contract (2.17, scope `build`, regenerated into `contracts-build.md`):** one scrollbar, drawn once; MUST NOT draw or switch a scrollbar at a scroller; the 64px and opt-out rule; a thumb only where its scroller is what is under it; the fade stays; the live pass runs with scrollbars drawn. **`AUTO §2.17`** in `scripts/check-contracts.mjs`: the global rule and the mount must exist, and `scrollbar-width`, `::-webkit-scrollbar`, `scrollbar-color`, `scrollbarWidth` or `overflow: overlay` in `app/pages` or `components` fails (shown to fail on a put-back rule, then restored). 4.2f: the lane is 0 now, so the `scrollbar-gutter: stable` it asks for costs nothing and is kept (nothing loosened, 9.4); its note that the lane "cannot be seen in the headless browser" is replaced by the pass above.
  - **Documented:** `/principles/craft` (the scroll detail), `/patterns/map-list-card` (the list rule), comments in `scroll-fade.tsx`, `map-surface.ts`, `area-layers.tsx`, `result-list.tsx`.
  - **Checked live (Playwright with scrollbars drawn, `ignoreDefaultArgs: ["--hide-scrollbars"]`; zero console errors):**
    - **79 static screens** (every `screen-index` path without a parameter or the auth flow), BioData Super Admin at 1440x800: 63 scrollers found, 62 of them 64px or more; none keeps a native lane (the one flagged, `/pages/projectsv2`, is a 1px right border read as a lane); hovering the biggest scroller of each screen showed a thumb on all 54 where it was a vertical one with no opt-out. `/pages/projects` scrolls the window sideways by design (a 1600px frame).
    - **Behaviour:** Explore's results list (thumb 6 wide, at the card's edge, gone 1.7s after the pointer left; the fade and the pinned Show N more unchanged); the report table (vertical thumb, and a horizontal one along its foot, hidden where the Prototype tools bar covers part of it); the parks multiselect's list (thumb inside the popover); a docs page that scrolls the window (thumb at the window's edge); dragging 60px of thumb moved the table from 100 to 482; the thumb is darker under the pointer (181,178,175 to 143,139,135); with touch the thumb takes no pointer events.
    - **Cost:** about 1.5s of wheel scrolling on the report: no long task, no frame over 34ms.
    - `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on every touched file, `npm run check:contracts` clean.
  - **Not checked:** a modal with its own scrolling body (the guard for what is over a thumb was seen with the tools bar, not a modal), Safari and Firefox (`scrollbar-width` and `::-webkit-scrollbar` are both set, so both should be off; the thumb uses pointer events and `elementFromPoint`), a scroller that is partly clipped by a rounded ancestor, a screen reader (the thumbs are `aria-hidden`; the scrollers are not announced as scrollable regions, as before), a session on Windows with the system set to always show scroll bars (the native bar is off by CSS there too, but this was checked in Chromium on macOS).
  - **Open, for the designer:**
    1. **Hover shows it whenever the pointer rests on a scroller,** as on the Explore lists. On a full page that is a thumb at the window's edge for as long as the pointer is over the page. If that is too much on page columns, the rule can be "while scrolling, and over the thumb's own edge strip", for page-sized scrollers only.
    2. **What a custom thumb gives up:** no click on a track to page, no OS styling, nothing for assistive technology. Wheel, touch and keyboard scrolling are the browser's. A "Show scroll bars: Always" setting is no longer honoured, since the browser's bar is off.
    3. **The Prototype tools bar** hides a thumb it covers. It is Scaffold, so product screens do not have it; on a prototype it can hide a table's horizontal thumb while the bar is over it.
    4. **Labs under `app/proto`** still have two hand-made `scrollbar-width: none` strips (a thumbnail strip and the notifications popover's chips); labs never ship (5.4), so they were left and are outside the check.
