# 2026-10-14 - Newsletter infographic answers to its width: stacked at email width, in rows from 768px, 1040px route

- **Oct 14 2026: Newsletter infographic answers to its width: stacked at email width, in rows from 768px, 1040px route.** Follows `2026-10-14-03`. The designer: "What if we weren't too strict about width?"
  - **What changed:** `TopInfographic` now lays itself out by the width it is given (Tailwind container queries, `@container` on the card, `@3xl:` for 768px and up). At email width (600px) nothing changes: the same stacked card, 1,218px tall. From 768px it lays out in rows: the vision's tagline (30px) beside its objective on the dark band; the five problems in one row of five tiles (a single grid, with column spans so the narrow version keeps its three-and-two rows); the six opportunity areas in one row of six. Same content, same colour bands, same tiles.
  - **New route `/nltemplate/infographic`:** the block alone at 1040px wide, nothing else on the page, so it can be captured as a picture. Measured there: 762px tall (vision 168, problems 282, opportunity areas 310), 37% shorter than the email-width 1,218px and 56% shorter than the first version (1,727px). A 1040 x 762 picture has the proportions of a slide, and the problems and opportunities read as two rows of icons, which is what an infographic is.
  - **The cost of width, said plainly:** an email is read at 600px. A 1040px picture placed in one is scaled to 58%, so the 12px column text becomes 7px and is unreadable on a phone (and the figure and label type go small too). So the wide version suits a web page, a PDF, a slide or a link from the email ("view the full infographic"); the email itself keeps the 600px stacked block. If the picture must sit inside the email, it should be built for 600px (what option 7 shows now).
  - **Verified (Playwright, port 3001):** the wide route renders at 1040px, no console errors; option 7 still 1,218px at email width, unchanged; `tsc`, `eslint`, `check:contracts`.
