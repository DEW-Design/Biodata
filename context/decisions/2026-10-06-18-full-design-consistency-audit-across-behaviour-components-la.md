# 2026-10-06 - Full design consistency audit across behaviour, components, layout and copy

- **Oct 6 2026: Full design consistency audit across behaviour, components, layout and copy.** The designer asked for a deep audit so the whole webapp feels like one product: behaviour patterns, components, visual layout, and copy down to Sentence case against Title Case. Method: the `design-consistency` skill plus six parallel read-only lanes (copy, type and colour, layout, components, behaviour, records and IA), each told the decisions already settled and the twelve themes the designer keeps correcting (dancing layouts, hand-built lookalikes, same-role-different-type, icon rules, information in column 2, record pages not following the project page, controls on the wrong side, All/My order, naming drift, brevity, empty states, case and punctuation).

The lanes made well over 1,200 live page loads (four roles, three widths for layout) with zero console errors, and produced 146 findings that merge into 44 root issues: 23 high, 16 medium, 5 low. I independently re-checked the load-bearing ones: 7 inline `role === "biodata-admin"` checks, the blank voucher deep link (0 characters in `main`), the header growing from 65px to 104px at 1280px wide on deep pages, the dead `border-error` / `border-brand` / `text-success-primary` classes in the upload dropzone, and `"Data Licencing Agreement (DLA)"` in the rail constant.

  **Headline.** The shell geometry, record hero, table card, form footer, menus and modals are consistent. The inconsistency is in words (no casing rule, one concept with several names, dates as "Sep" and "Sept", seven wordings for an empty value), in the same role drawn two or three ways (label/value rows, table cells, status colours, card radius), in states never made into one component (empty, not found, restricted, discard, view switch), and in record pages built before the pattern.
