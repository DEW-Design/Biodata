# 2026-10-23 - Ghost controls: the drawn box is the touch target (ghost-fill); Explore's list heading with Export data

- **Oct 23 2026: Ghost controls: the drawn box is the touch target (`ghost-fill`); Explore's list heading with Export data.** The designer, with a screenshot of the Explore card as it was laid out before its second layout was retired (a "SPECIES" heading at the left of the list and "EXPORT DATA" at the right, under the tabs): "What I meant by being consistent for the species tab", and, with a close crop of "Hide list" pointed at, its fill and focus ring hugging the text: "make the padding of ghost buttons consistent with the touch target sizes." The message ended at "Also," with nothing after it; it is asked about, not guessed.
  - **The list heading (Explore).** Map visualise's list opens with a heading row (the category and its count at the left, the name of the column of badges at the right). Explore's did not, and its export was an icon on the count line. Now every Explore tab's list opens with the same row: the tab's name at the left in the eyebrow layer (12/600 uppercase, quaternary) and **Export data** at the right, a ghost button with the download icon, which opens the same CSV, Excel and PDF menu (a signed-out visitor gets the same sign-up tooltip and prompt). The count stays on the tab; the heading does not repeat it. The heading is now one shared part (`map-search/chunk-heading.tsx`) used by Map visualise and Explore, with the trailing slot open to a control, and a `roomy` form for a control. **A difference from the screenshot, for the designer to settle:** the screenshot sets "EXPORT DATA" in the eyebrow type; it is drawn here as the design system's ghost button (14/600, sentence case, with its icon), because a button's own text is not restyled at a call site (2.9). If the eyebrow look is wanted, it is a variant of the ghost button to add once, not a class on this one. **Folding the list hides the heading, so Export data is hidden with it** (it used to stay on the count line as an icon).
  - **Ghost padding (`ghost-fill`, `app/globals.css`).** The ghost button's fill and focus ring were 24px high inside an invisible 40px target (padding `6px 2px`, a hit area behind it). The padding is now what makes the target: `(--hit-min - 20px) / 2` on all four sides, so 10px with a mouse and 12px with touch, never under 6px; the same equal negative margin gives it back, so the text does not move (the first lines of the sign-in screen sit where they sat). It follows `--hit-min`, so a group that caps the target (`[--hit-min:32px]`) caps the fill with it. The radius is 6px (it was 6 before; the first try at 8 was wrong), which is concentric at a card's 12px corner with the 6px between the card's edge and the back link's fill (2.13). Applied through the one utility to: Link grey (`Button`), the record pages' `RecordBackLink`, the `BreadcrumbSwitcher` trigger and every header crumb. Not applied to the profile trigger (an avatar, already 40px or more) or to icon-only buttons (their own sizes). `hit-area` was dropped from the three hand-built ones, since their box is the target; Link grey keeps it (inert there).
  - **Room.** A 40px box needs 10px of clear space past a 20px line, or its fill runs over its neighbours. Explore's count line and list heading were tight, so Explore's tab strip takes `mt-1.5 mb-0` as Map visualise's does, and the heading takes 10px above and below (`roomy`). Measured: the "Hide list" ring clears the tab strip, and the Export data fill clears the tab underline and the first row.
  - **Contract.** CONTRACTS 2.16 has a new bullet (a ghost control's drawn box is its target, `ghost-fill`, with the room of its box, never padding and negative margin by hand); `AUTO §2.16c` fails if a file that draws a ghost control (`button.tsx`, `record-hero.tsx`, `breadcrumb-switcher.tsx`, `app-header.tsx`) loses the class, comments stripped (proved: removing it from `button.tsx` fails the check; restored). Also in `ref-ingest.md` (QA 11), the Button doc page and `/principles/craft`.
