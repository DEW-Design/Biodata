# 2026-10-16 - Taxonomy audit logs: the Action filter always offers rename, combine, split and append, and mock entries

- **Oct 16 2026: two follow-ups to `2026-10-16-05-*`.** The designer: "I just need the combine, rename, split and append to be added to the actions filter. Let's also create some mock data, this is just to show when a particular species' taxonomy was either combined, renamed, split and/or appended."

  **The Action filter.** The audit log's Action attribute (Flora Taxonomy Report and Fauna Taxonomy Report) was read from the rows, so it only offered an action once a row had it. It now always offers Append, Combine, Create, Rename, Split and Update (`AUDIT_ACTIONS` in `taxonomy-audit-data.ts`, joined with whatever the rows hold), so choosing one that has no row yet shows the usual "No events match" state with its way back.

  **Mock entries.** `taxonomy-audit-mock.ts` holds four made-up events for each report, one of each action, written exactly like a change committed in Taxonomy Management (one row for each name it made; Concept, the rank, Previous and Current details, Portal, BioData Admin): Flora, Eucalyptus leucoxylon renamed to subsp. pruinosa (12 Aug 2026, P01937), Xanthorrhoea semiplana split into subsp. semiplana and subsp. tateana (3 Sep, P04412 and P04413), two Acacia pycnantha subspecies combined into Acacia pycnantha (22 Sep, P09999), Eucalyptus camaldulensis appended (8 Oct, P01938); Fauna, Isoodon obesulus renamed to subsp. obesulus (19 Aug, M01170), Trichosurus vulpecula split into subsp. vulpecula and subsp. fuliginosus (9 Sep, M01131 and M01132), Crinia signifera and Crinia parinsignifera combined into Crinia signifera (28 Sep, A00215), Tachyglossus aculeatus subsp. multiaculeatus appended (12 Oct, M01003). The people are the placeholder people (Lana Steiner, Phoenix Baker, Olivia Wyatt, Maya Dewitt); the species are the sample taxa and well-known Australian species, but when each was changed and the NSX codes of the species a change made are made up (CONTRACTS 0.3). They are shown beside any real change and are never stored, so Taxonomy Management's change history and Linked Species do not show them. The Flora log now has 9 rows and the Fauna log 15; choosing Split leaves 2 in each. Searching a code (P04411, M01170) finds the taxon's rows on every tab.

  **Open for the designer (CONTRACTS 0.4):** (1) the mock entries are in the report only; say if a committed change in Taxonomy Management should also write a row (it already does for a real Rename, Combine, Split or Append). (2) The NSX codes of the made species are made up and should be replaced when the real list arrives.
