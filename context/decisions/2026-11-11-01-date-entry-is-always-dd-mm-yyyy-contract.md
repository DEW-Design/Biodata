# 2026-11-11 - Date entry is always DD/MM/YYYY (contract 2.11 amended)

- **Nov 11 2026: a date is entered day, month, year, whatever the browser's language.** The designer: "DATE ENTRY FORMAT WILL ALWAYS BE DD/MM/YYYY". Amends `CONTRACTS.md` 2.11 (an addition, nothing loosened; `.claude/rules/contracts-build.md` regenerated) and adds `AUTO §2.11b` to `scripts/check-contracts.mjs` (a change to the contract files, flagged in the audit, 9.4).
  **The fault.** Both date fields (`InputDatePicker`, `InputDate`) let react-aria pick the segment order from the browser's locale, under a header comment that said DD/MM/YYYY. Measured at 1440x1000 with Playwright on the Controlled Vocabulary form: an `en-US` browser drew month, day, year; `en-AU` and `en-GB` drew day, month, year.
  **The fix, once, in the components (1.10).** `utils/date-entry-locale.ts` holds `DATE_ENTRY_LOCALE = "en-AU"`; each field renders inside `<I18nProvider locale={DATE_ENTRY_LOCALE}>` (the picker through a thin `InputDatePicker` over `DatePickerField`, `InputDate` over `DateFieldRoot`), so every screen gets it and none sets it. The calendar's week starts on Monday with the locale. The check fails if either field loses the provider or the locale is not a day-first one.
  **Verified.** `tsc`, `eslint` on the touched files and `check:contracts` pass. In an `en-US` browser the fields now draw day, month, year; typing `25122026` into the End date field reads 25 / 12 / 2026 (impossible in month-first order); the calendar opens on December 2026 with Monday first; zero console errors. Sibling grep: every date a person enters in `app` and `components` goes through `InputDatePicker` (no text date inputs, no `type="date"`); displayed dates already use `en-AU`; the ingestion reads files day first. **Not checked:** each of the ~25 screens with a date field (they share the one component), touch, a screen reader announcing the order.
  **Not changed.** How a date is shown in text ("1 Mar 2026"), which 2.3 and the record decide.
  **Undo.** Remove the two `I18nProvider` wrappers, `utils/date-entry-locale.ts`, the 2.11 bullets and `AUTO §2.11b`; the fields then follow the browser again. Not committed.
