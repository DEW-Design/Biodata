Can we clean this up? Also, what if we had a carousel of images? I've added a new carousel component from untitled ui, can you please follow the component ingestion flow to properly bring this over into the design system, then into the said instance?"
  - **Principles (0.10):** 1 Say less (a credit nobody asked for is a caption under every photo; it is behind an icon until asked), 3 One system (one carousel, in `components/**`, and the three screens that show a species photo take it from the one place), 4 Tell the truth (every extra photo is a real ALA image with a recorded licence, none invented), 6 Reveal in steps, 8 Reach everyone (where they pull: below).

  - **The credit:** an info icon (`ButtonUtility`, extra small, round) in the photo's bottom-right corner. Pointing at it or tabbing to it shows the DS tooltip, "Photo: Robert Browne-Cooper, CC BY, Atlas of Living Australia", and Escape closes it; the same sentence is its accessible name; pressing it opens the photo's own page on ALA in a new tab (where the full licence sits), so the link the caption had is kept. In a carousel it is always the credit of the photo showing (checked: "Photo: Rueben, CC BY-NC, ..." after Next).
  - **Where two principles pull apart, and what was chosen:** Say less wants the credit out of sight; Reach everyone and Tell the truth want it reachable by a keyboard and a thumb, and a licence credit is a duty, not decoration. A tooltip cannot hold a link (a link in one is unreachable from the keyboard), so the icon is the link and the tooltip is the text. **The gap this leaves, for the designer:** on a touch screen a tooltip does not open, so a tap on the icon goes straight to the photo's page on ALA instead of showing the credit first. A press-to-open popover with the credit and a working link would close it, but it is a new component (1.4) and was not built unasked.
  - **Real photos for the carousel (0.3):** the Bandicoot had one photo, so there was nothing to page through. Three more were taken from the Atlas of Living Australia's records of *Isoodon obesulus* in South Australia that have images, kept to the rule in `species-images.ts` (a recorded licence that allows display with credit: CC BY or CC BY-NC; here all CC BY-NC 4.0, from iNaturalist Australia): Rueben, Glenn Gale and Bijoy Venugopal, as 650px copies in `public/pages/species/` like the others. Looked at, not taken: a dead animal, a blurred one, a boot, and two where the animal is barely in the frame. The ALA image ids are in `species-images.ts`. Only this species has more than one photo.
