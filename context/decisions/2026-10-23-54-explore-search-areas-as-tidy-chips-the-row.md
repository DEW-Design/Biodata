# 2026-10-23 - Explore search areas as tidy chips: the row controls taken out for now

- **Oct 23 2026: Explore search areas as tidy chips: the row controls taken out for now.** The designer, with a picture of the card whose "Search results 54" row is followed by three small chips ("Circle 1 x", "Circle 2 x", "Polygon 1 x"): "what if we keep search layers nice and tidy like this? remove all the bs controls for now?"
  - **What an area is now:** one removable chip per area you add (a drawn circle or polygon, an entered point, a national park, a shapefile), under the "Search results" heading, with the icon of what it is and its name. The design system's own `Tag` with its X, the chip the table filters use (`AttributeFilterChips`). The X removes it (a click, or Delete on a focused chip); New search clears them all. Pointing at a chip outlines its shape on the map, as the rows' hover did (the stroke goes from 2 to 4, measured, and goes back on leaving). Chips wrap, and from a fifth line they scroll inside 112px (a drawn thumb and a fade, `ScrollFade`), so the results below keep their room (11 chips measured: the list 112px in a 729px card).
  - **What was taken out (so it can come back, from this list and git):** per area, the eye that hid it from the search (and the "every area hidden" state that went with it), its radius and record count ("25 km radius, 26 records"), the "..." menu (Rename, Change radius, Remove), the name as a button that zoomed to the area, and the "Clear all" above the list. In the code: the `hiddenLayerIds` and `radiusOverrides` state (and their two fields in the saved search, which an old saved search may still carry and which are now ignored), the rename, radius, hide, zoom and per-area count handlers (`countInBoundaries`), `fitRequest`, the old `AreaLayerList` row component, and the "whole-state" row. **Kept:** the generated names ("Circle 1", "Polygon 1", "Point 1"), through `layerNames` and its counters.
  - **A keyword with no area** searches the whole state and used to show a row, "All of South Australia", whose bin icon cleared the keyword. It has no chip now: the search field says the keyword (and has its own X) and the heading counts the results. The heading and Hide list still show for it.
  - **The Tag, two additive props (1.6):** `icon` (an icon before the label, opt-in) and `onHoverChange` (told when the pointer is over the tag; react-aria's own hover is off for a tag that is not selectable, so it is heard on a wrapper with no box of its own). Nothing changes for a Tag that does not pass them: the table filter chips measured the same after (136 by 20px, the X in the same place, "STATUS: DRAFT").
  - **Differences from the picture, for the designer to settle:** (1) **The look.** The picture's chips are larger, in sentence case and regular weight on a grey fill; the design system's `Tag` is 20px high, small caps-style (semibold, uppercase), white with a ring, as every filter chip in the product. Built with the real component, not redrawn (1.1, 2.9). If the picture's look is wanted, it is a Tag size or variant to add once and the filter chips would take it too. (2) **The icon.** The picture has the same circle in front of all three, including "Polygon 1". An icon on every chip that is the same says nothing (2.12), so each takes the icon of its kind, the same icons the Add area menu uses (circle, pentagon, a pin for a point, a map for a park, an upload for a shapefile); the circle is the picture's.
  - **What is lost for now, said plainly:** you cannot hide one area and keep it, rename it, change a circle's radius (delete it and draw or enter another) or see how many records each area holds (the heading has the total).
  - **Checked live (Playwright, Super Admin; zero console problems):** a drawn circle, a drawn polygon and an entered point show as CIRCLE 1, POLYGON 1 and POINT 1 with their icons; no eye, no menu and no Clear all remain; X removes one; Delete removes a focused one; ten more points give 11 chips in a list of 112px with its thumb; New search clears every chip and the heading; a keyword alone shows the heading and the two tabs and no chip. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the touched files and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
  - **Not checked:** Safari, a screen reader on the chip's X (the design system's close button is labelled "Remove this tag" on every chip, not the area's name: a gap in the Tag itself, worth fixing there), a saved search from before this change that had hidden areas (the field is ignored, so they show again).
