# 2026-10-23 - Area chips use the large Tag

- **Oct 23 2026: Area chips use the large Tag.** The designer, with a crop of a chip ("CIRCLE 7 x", the circle larger than the capitals, the padding tight): "this tag looks awful, can you use a bigger tag from the design system?"
  - **The change:** Explore's area chips (`AreaLayerChips`) take the design system's **large** `Tag` (`size="lg"`) instead of the medium one the table filter chips use. Measured: 28px high and 14/600 where the medium was 20px high; the icon 16px (it was 16px beside 14px text in a 20px chip, which is why it looked too big); the close X 14px; 9px of padding before the icon, 4px after the X; 6px corners. It is the largest size the Tag has (sm, md, lg).
  - **One fix in the Tag so the icon keeps its proportion (additive, opt-in prop only):** the `icon` is 12px in a small tag, 14px in a medium one and 16px in a large one (it was 12px, then 16px for both). The filter chips have no icon, so nothing else changed (they were not touched: they stay medium).
  - **A consequence:** a chip is taller, so the list that scrolls from a certain number of chips now shows three lines (3 x 28px and two 8px gaps is 100px, in a 112px list) before it scrolls with its drawn thumb; the comment says so. `2026-10-23-54` said four lines at the medium size; this replaces it.
  - **Not changed:** the Tag's own look, uppercase and semibold (the same as every chip in the product), and its close button label ("Remove this tag" on every chip). If a still bigger or sentence-case chip is wanted, it is a new Tag size or variant for the design system, and every filter chip would take it.
  - **Checked live (Playwright, Super Admin; zero console errors):** two points and a drawn circle show as 28px chips with their icons at 16px (looked at in a 3x screenshot); 11 chips give three lines in a 112px list with its thumb; X, Delete and the hover outline on the map still work; New search and a keyword-only search are unchanged. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the touched files and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
