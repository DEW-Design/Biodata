# 2026-10-23 - Contract 2.16: a control's hit area is built into its component

- **Oct 23 2026: Contract 2.16: a control's hit area is built into its component.** The designer, answering the three questions `2026-10-23-41-*` ended on: "Yes, please. Make sure these are documented in contracts." The three were: make it a contract, with checks; accept that the hit area is on by default (CONTRACTS 1.6 says an addition to a component is opt-in); and replace the hand-built lookalikes with the components that carry it.
  - **Added (`CONTRACTS.md`, generated into `.claude/rules/contracts-build.md` and the core index with `npm run contracts:rules`; scope `build` in `contracts/rule-scopes.json`):** **2.16 "A control's hit area is built into its component"**. A control a person presses has a target of at least 40px with a mouse and 44px on touch, given by the component once with `hit-area` (`app/globals.css`). It MUST be on the root of a component that renders a control smaller than 40px (the components listed in the clause, and a new one of that kind; the header gives it to every breadcrumb link once); a screen MUST NOT enlarge a target with a `before:` or `after:` of its own, or draw a small button, icon button, back link or close icon by hand where `Button`, `ButtonUtility`, `CloseButton` or `RecordBackLink` does the job; two targets never overlap (a group caps the extension with `[--hit-min:32px]` or turns it off with `after:content-none` or `data-no-hit-area`), and it is not for a full-width field, a stacked row or a chip's remove icon; a container that scrolls or clips around a control gives it room (`ScrollFade` and the four strips do, `pb-1.5 -mb-1.5`); a bordered control sets `--hit-dx` or `--hit-dy`; a control is never `static`. Checked live by pressing just inside and just outside, with a mouse and with touch emulated, and by listing any scroller that grew.
  - **The 1.6 exception, recorded where the rule is (designer, 23 Oct 2026):** 1.6 now carries a bullet saying the hit area is on by default in every component that carries it, because it changes what a press hits and nothing about how a control looks or sits, and the instruction was that every screen has it. This is a loosening of 1.6 by an explicit designer decision (9.4), named here and in the clause.
  - **Two automatic checks (`scripts/check-contracts.mjs`; tightening, so allowed under 9.4, and flagged by the audit as a change to the checker):** **`AUTO 2.16a`**: each of the twelve components that carry the hit area (`Button`, `ButtonUtility`, `CloseButton`, `Tab`, `Checkbox`, `RadioButton`, `Toggle`, `Input`, the table's pagination, the dropdown's "..." button, `TooltipTrigger`, the date picker) fails the check if its file loses `hit-area`. **`AUTO 2.16b`**: a hard rule over `app/pages`: a negative-inset `before:` or `after:` (`before:-inset-*`, `after:-top-*` and the rest) fails, with no allowance. It found four hand-written ones, now converted (below); the one in `tree-view.tsx` is a drop line and is outside `app/pages`. Both pass.
  - **The lookalikes, as far as this pass went:** the observation page's own "Back to Adelaide Hills Bushland Survey" link is now `RecordBackLink` (its container, `p-6 pb-0`, was `RecordBackLink`'s own `px-6 pt-6`, so nothing moved); four hand-written enlargers became `hit-area`: the threat card's pin button, the area list's eye button (kept at 36px with `[--hit-min:36px]`, as it was), the photo carousel's arrow chips and the summary card's close button (now one shared `SummaryCloseButton`, see `-43`). **Not replaced, and why:** the star and menu on a report card (`FavouriteButton` and `Dropdown.DotsButton`: made `ButtonUtility` they would be 4px bigger and gain a fill beside a menu button that has none, a visible change on every report card), the lightbox arrows and the zoom buttons (designs of their own), about 70 more hand-built buttons that are rows, tiles, tabs and cards rather than small buttons, and the draft and lab screens. They are each a design question and not a hit-area one; the check does not ask for them.
  - **Verified:** `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on every touched and new file, and `npm run check:contracts` (with the two new checks) clean; the audit of 80 screens with a mouse and touch: no new overlap and no scroller that grew; every hit-area control keeps the position its own classes ask for (one differs by design, the alert's close button from the md breakpoint). Not committed.
  - **Open:** none beyond the lookalikes above; the clause names a first occurrence and is `REVIEW` apart from the two checks.
