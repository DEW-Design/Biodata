# 2026-10-23 - Explore empty card: the guidance line is the field's scope-first hint

- **Oct 23 2026: Explore empty card: the guidance line is the field's scope-first hint.** The designer, with a screenshot of the empty Explore card ("EXPLORE / Search the map", "+ Add area", the field, and under it "Add an area, or search a species across South Australia."): "let's tweak the add an area, species etc. text. Hierarchy is failing here. Can we rearrange it in a way that properly fits a normal user's mental model? /refine-prompt" (the refinement, its vocabulary table and its prompt were shown with the reply).
  - **What failed (Principle 2 and 4.3):** the line under the field restated, in a sentence, what the field's placeholder and the Add area button already say, and put them in the wrong order for how a person works (it led with "Add an area", the secondary step, before the search, the first one). It was 14px tertiary grey, darker than the field's own placeholder (`rgb(181, 178, 175)`), so on a card with nothing in it the instruction outranked the field. "Add an area" in a sentence also looked pressable and was not (the real button is at the top right: a missing affordance, the user guesses).
  - **The picture a person has:** type what you want; it looks everywhere unless you narrow it. So the field says *what*, the button says *how to narrow*, and the one fact the card did not show is *where it looks if you do nothing*. The line now says only that, scope first, with no imperative: **"Covers all of South Australia until you add an area."**
  - **How:** it is the field's own hint (`hint` of the design system `Input`, which renders `HintText` under the field) and not a separate paragraph beside it: one thing with the field, in the type the product's field hints have (measured 14/400, 20px line, `rgb(112, 107, 104)`), and described to a screen reader as the field's description (checked: the field's `aria-describedby` points at it). Under 2.9 nothing new in type was made. The separate `<p>`, its id and the manual `aria-describedby` are gone.
  - **When it shows (as before):** the card empty, with no area, no word and no add form open. It goes the moment a word is typed (checked: gone while typing, back when the field is cleared) or an area or a form is open; from then on the heading counts the results and the chips say where.
  - **Checked live (Playwright, Super Admin; zero console errors):** at 1686px and 720px; typing, clearing and opening Enter coordinates. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the file and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
  - **What this does not do, said plainly:** the line is still 14px grey and the card still has the brand-coloured **Add area** at the top right, which is the highest-contrast thing on it after the title and weighs more than the field's faint placeholder. That is the bigger lever for hierarchy, and it is the designer's own redesign (`2026-10-23-48`), so it was not touched. Two ways to go, for the designer: (1) keep Add area at the top right and quiet it (the grey ghost, not the brand one), so the field is first; (2) re-home it beside the field as a "where" row ("All of South Australia" with + Add area at its right, becoming the chips when there are areas), so the card reads what, then where. Also open: whether the eyebrow EXPLORE, which repeats the breadcrumb ("Home / Explore"), stays.
