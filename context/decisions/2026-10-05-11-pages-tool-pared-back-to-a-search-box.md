# 2026-10-05 - Pages tool pared back to a search box, the tree and one line

- **Oct 5 2026: the Pages panel (`2026-10-05-09-*`, `-10-*`) was cut down to what the person needs. From the designer, with a screenshot of an analytics dashboard from /interface-craft whose summary reads "deliberate choices to remove redundancy, simplify visual choices, and allow the data to speak for itself": "Less is more."**
  - **Removed:** the violet header band (title, "N pages for <role>"), the Tree/Search view switch and the whole Search list (the search box already filters the tree and opens every match, so the second view repeated it), the page count on every area, the lock and the reasons (gone since pages are hidden), the tree's connector lines, the violet footer band with its sentence and six persona buttons, and the panel's violet ring. The panel is 360px wide, down from 440.
  - **Kept:** one search box, the DEW `TreeView` (no checkboxes), the page you are on marked and scrolled into view with its ancestors open, only the persona's pages (`2026-10-05-10-*`), keyboard (Down from the box into the tree, Enter in the box to the first match). An area of one screen (Landing page, Home, Template Finder) is now that screen as a plain row, aligned with the area labels, instead of a group holding one row.
  - **Where switching persona went:** one quiet line at the foot: "43 pages" on the left and the persona's name with a chevron on the right. The name opens a menu of all seven personas, current one ticked, each with its page count (72, 64, 43, 43, 43, 43, 22), so the numbers appear only when asked for. Choosing one switches in place (the shared `useSetRole`, `2026-10-05-10-*`). This is less prominent than the six buttons it replaces; if switching needs to be more inviting, the count line is the place to push it.
  - **Verified live (Playwright, 1708x1024, zero console errors):** as Registered User the panel lists Landing page, Sign in, Home, Projects, Explore, Data licence, Nominations, Template Finder, Reports, Unlinked; the persona menu lists seven with counts; choosing BioData Admin keeps the panel open on the same page and the foot reads "64 pages"; typing "nomination" and Enter opens `/pages/nominations?userRole=biodata-admin`. `tsc`, eslint and `check:contracts` pass.
  - **Not done:** the Emil typography pass, a keyboard-only pass of the persona menu, narrow windows. The lab `/proto/site-map` still holds the earlier, heavier layouts and is unchanged.
  - **Open for the designer:** whether to keep the lab now that the bar has the panel (recommend deleting it, 4.4); whether the persona line is inviting enough; the points from `-10-` still open (Sign in pages for signed-in personas, identical counts for four personas, the 3.8 line, the screen-index route check).
