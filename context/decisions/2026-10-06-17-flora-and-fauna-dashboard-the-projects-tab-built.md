# 2026-10-06 - Flora and Fauna Dashboard: the Projects tab built from the reference

- **Oct 6 2026: the Projects tab of the Flora and Fauna Dashboard (Home) was the "hasn't been scoped yet" placeholder. Built from the designer's screenshot of the live SA Flora and Fauna Data Dashboard's Projects tab ("data dashboard - project").**
  - **Built (`projects-content.tsx`, wired in `data-overview.tsx`; the placeholder is removed):** the same card shell, metric card and pie chart as Overview and Flora (`BentoCard`, `MetricCard`, `PieChart`), laid out like Flora's tab: the **Total number of projects** (1,435), **Survey type** (Fauna survey 41%, Flora and fauna survey 28%, Flora survey 26%, Other survey 6%) and **Number of projects per mapsheet** (the map) on top; **Data sensitivity** (Publicly available 89%, Only available under licence 11%), **Data accessibility** (Projects with data entered 70%, without 30%) and the statement **126 projects have captured 7,236 photopoints at 4,048 locations** below; a closing line with a real link to the Projects list.
  - **Where the numbers come from:** the screenshot, not this app's four sample projects, the same convention as the Overview and Flora tabs (1,435 is also the Overview's "Projects across SA"). The reference's survey-type slices add to 101% because it rounds each; they are shown as it shows them. Nothing was invented.
  - **Departures from the reference, on purpose:** (1) its Filters panel (Landscape SA region, National Parks and Wildlife region) is not built: nothing in the app gives a project a region for the totals to be filtered by, so the controls could not change a number (0.3 no fabrication, and 4.2d's "no attribute the data cannot filter on"); the other tabs have none either; (2) the map is the real Australian map the other tabs use with South Australia highlighted, not a per-mapsheet heat map, because no mapsheet boundaries are bundled (`map-view.tsx` says so); (3) pie colours are the design system's categorical palette (brand and neutral for two-slice splits), not the reference's blues, as Flora does.
  - **Verified live (Playwright, 1708x1100, zero console errors):** the tab shows on Home as Registered User, Public user and BioData Admin, with every block and no placeholder; the "Open the Project list" link goes to `/pages/project-list` with the role kept. `tsc`, eslint on the two files and `check:contracts` pass.
  - **Not done:** the Emil typography pass (the text takes its classes from Flora's cards: `text-sm font-semibold` titles, the metric number and uppercase caption), narrow widths, and a keyboard pass of the pie legends (they are plain lists).
  - **Open for the designer:** whether the reference's two region filters should be added with a region on each project (it needs data the app does not hold); whether the Projects tab's numbers should one day come from the real project list. Not committed.
