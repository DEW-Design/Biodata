Both default to the old drawing, so no other tree changes (the records explorers, the taxonomy finder and the observation page do not pass them). Documented on `/patterns/tree-view` (a new example, a paragraph and a note). In the panel, areas and top-level screens are semibold and screens inside an area are normal weight, measured 600 against 400 with labels 24px in (1313 to 1337).
  - **New, approved: Recent.** Above "All pages", up to four screens the person opened last that this persona can open, not the one they are on, in the same tree styling at normal weight. Panel scroll now keeps the current page in view with `block: "nearest"` (it looks for the row over a few frames, the tree builds its rows after mount) so Recent stays visible.
