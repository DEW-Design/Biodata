# 2026-10-30 - Project page: Cancel and Save changes sit in the card being edited, and the Geographic extent card is one shell for every project

- **Oct 30 2026: two changes on the project page's Overview, both from one screenshot.** The designer, with the Geographic extent card in edit mode, the page footer at the foot of the window reading "Editing Geographic extent" at the far left and Cancel / Save changes at the far right: "easy to miss, bad UX." and "add these back into each editing section, please and thank you." And, of the same card in view mode: "Adelaide Hills bushland project has geographic context layout slightly different to the one we just created. The location table on the left and the square map on the right ... The shell for project details has to be consistent. no exceptions." Then, on seeing a table-and-square-map built at 24rem and then at half the card: "Hate it - a 50-50 split for the map would be better", and "Hate how big the map is now looking - can we please make sure the height of the map on the right matches with the table on the left?"
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 5 Make it obvious (the controls that end an edit are where the edit is), 3 One system (every project, every kind of extent, the same card), 7 Design every state (a circle, a polygon, a place, a shapefile name, nothing), 1 Say less (the footer repeated the card's own title). They agree.
  - **Override of CONTRACTS 4.8 item 4 (0.1), named and by the designer's instruction.** Item 4 said Cancel and Save changes sit in one sticky footer across the bottom of the page and "MUST NOT put them inside a card". For the project page, whose cards are edited one at a time, they now sit at the foot of the card being edited. CONTRACTS 4.8 item 4 is rewritten in place: a page that edits one card at a time has the pair in the card; a page that edits every card at once (a record's "Edit record") keeps the one sticky footer; the pair is never drawn twice on a page. The generated rules (`contracts-shell.md`) are rebuilt. Not recorded in `contracts/overrides.json`: that register is for new components (1.4, 9.2).
  - **What changed, the footer (`project-tab.tsx`, `project-edit.tsx`).** The page-level footer element is gone, and with it the `footer` in the editing context. A card in edit mode renders its own slot at its foot (`sticky bottom-0`, running to the card's edges), and `InlineCardEditor` portals its bar into it. It is one pair per page: Cancel (`XClose`, secondary) and Save changes (`Save01`, primary; "Add restrictions" for a new restriction), with "Unsaved changes" at the left once there are any. "Editing <title>" is not repeated: the card's title is directly above, and 4.8 item 2 already says an edited card carries no "Editing" label. The bar's bottom corners are 11px inside the card's 12px radius with a 1px border (concentric, 2.13). Behaviour is as it was: Cancel with changes asks first, Save with a missing field shows the "Details missing" alert, and a restriction just added opens its own card.
  - **What changed, the Geographic extent card (`project-tab.tsx`).** It had two shapes: a circle (Adelaide Hills, any project drawn or chosen as a circle) was a location table, three rows (centre latitude, centre longitude, radius) and a map, and every other extent (a polygon, a shapefile) was one text row and no map. Now every extent that exists is the same: the location table on the left and the map on the right, half the card each, the map as tall as the table (261px at 1340px of card; measured equal on Adelaide Hills, a project made in the browser with a circle, and one with a polygon), then one "Extent" row under the pair (`drawnShapeLine`, the picker's own "Circle, 12 km radius", "15 km radius around Coorong National Park", "Polygon, 4 points"; a shapefile or a place with no known centre shows its name). The three rows are gone: two repeated the table's own latitude and longitude (4.3, never restate a fact in two treatments) and the radius is in the Extent row. Below the lg breakpoint the table is first and the map under it, 256px tall. With no extent at all it is still the one compact empty state with its action.
  - **A polygon has no point of its own.** Its table shows the centre of its vertices (`boundaryCentre`), which is how a record's latitude and longitude are already set from a drawn location. The table's column is headed "Entered Value", which for a polygon is a derived centre and not an entered one.
  - **An extent that is only a name (a shapefile) has no geometry,** so its table shows dashes (the table's own state for no coordinates) and its map is the state's map with no shape on it.
