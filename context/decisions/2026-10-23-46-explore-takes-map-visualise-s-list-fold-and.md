# 2026-10-23 - Explore takes Map visualise's list fold and tab icons (contract 3.13 exemption removed)

- **Oct 23 2026: Explore takes Map visualise's list fold and tab icons (contract 3.13 exemption removed).** The designer, with a screenshot of Explore's search card: "We'll need to make this consistent with what we have in the reports map visualise panel. We'll need to apply all the good stuff we did on the reports map visualise panel to the explore tab." The list polish (the shared `MapResultList`, `ResultCard`, `ScrollFade`, the summary lines, the key) had already been brought across (`2026-10-23-43`), so the two cards were measured side by side in a browser (both at 440px, same window) to find what still differed.
  - **What still differed, and what was done:**
    1. **Hide list / Show list.** Map visualise folds its card to its header so the map can be seen whole; Explore's card (about 770px tall in a 968px window) could not. Built once as `useListToggle` and `ListToggleButton` (`app/pages/_shared/map-search/list-toggle.tsx`): the card folds to what is above the list, the list is unmounted, its scroll position is kept and put back on unfolding, and a different list (another tab, another keyword, another count) unfolds at its top. Map visualise was moved onto the shared parts (its own copy of the state, effect and button is gone, 0.9). In Explore the button sits on the count line ("60 records across 1 area"), between the count and the download, so the line that names the results is always on screen. The card measured 771px open and 220px folded.
    2. **Icons on the tabs.** Map visualise's tabs carry icons (3.13); Explore's did not, because 3.13 exempted them (the 440px card needs about 820px with icons, measured, and scrolls sideways). Map visualise's tabs scroll in the same card, with the fade saying there is more, so the reason no longer holds. Species takes `Feather` (3.13's own mapping); Projects, Events, Occurrences, Observations and Artefacts and Attachments take the icons Figma's Metrics section draws, already in `entityTabs` (Folder, Activity, Target05, Eye, File06). **The 3.13 exemption is removed** from CONTRACTS.md, `ref-shell.md` and the `AUTO §3.13` check (`observations/observations-search.tsx` is no longer in its allow list). That tightens the check, not loosens it (9.4).
  - **Looked at and deliberately not brought across:**
    - **The group heading** (Map visualise's "BUSINESS RULE 54 ... SEVERITY" at the top of the list). It names a category and the column of badges. Explore's tab already names the kind and its count, and its rows have no column of badges, so a heading would say "Species 14" twice (2.12).
    - **The row's marker** (a coloured dot in Map visualise, a photo or species-group icon in Explore). The dot is the category's colour on the map and in the key; Explore's tile is the species group, which its key names. Each is that screen's own thing.
    - **The header** (a way back, the dataset, its file and project). It answers "where did I come from" for a screen reached from a report; Explore is the place itself.
  - **Open, for the designer: which name leads in Explore's species rows.** Map visualise leads with the scientific name in italics (2.15, because it derives from a table), Explore with the common name and the scientific name under it. 2.15 names Explore as out of scope (it is the source of its own list), so nothing was changed, but it is the one visible difference left between the two rows. Two readings: keep Explore common-name-first (a person searching the map thinks in common names); or lead with the scientific name everywhere (one rule, "if it shows scientific name first, then it's what should show up"). It is a decision about what Explore leads with, so it waits for the designer (0.4).
  - **Checked live (Playwright, BioData Super Admin; public and registered user for load):** all six Explore tabs carry an icon (widths 106 to 145px, the strip 822px in a 438px scroller, no vertical scroll: scrollHeight 44, clientHeight 44); the toggle's label, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` change with the state; the list is out of the DOM while folded; scroll 400 before, 400 after; a new keyword while folded unfolds at 0; Space and Enter on the toggle fold and unfold and focus stays on it; the toggle's hit area is 40px (mouse) and 44px (touch emulated) and the ghost fill is `rgb(248, 248, 247)` on hover; Map visualise's own fold still works (300 before, 300 after). Zero console errors. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the touched files and `npm run check:contracts` clean; `npm run contracts:rules` regenerated.
  - **Also checked:** a 720px window, where the panels stack and the card is 352px: the count line, the toggle and the download share 350px with no overflow, and the card folds to 248px. Screenshots of the card open, folded and narrow were looked at; the tabs' icons at the right edge (Occurrences onwards) are reached by scrolling, as on Map visualise.
  - **Not checked:** a real touch device, Safari.
