# 2026-10-13 - Post 6 Oct lab: the nested rail, from Notion

- **Oct 13 2026: Post 6 Oct lab: the nested rail, from Notion.** The designer, from three Notion sidebar screenshots (default, hover, expanded): "left side bar improvements specifically. I really love this pattern from Notion: default; on hover the page icon changes to a right chevron; on click it opens all hidden pages. This is also a very neat pattern." In `/proto/post-6-oct` only. The pattern is Notion's interaction (2.6: an idea, never its colour, spacing or type); the rows use the product's own tokens and Barlow.

  **The pattern, as built (`nested-rail.tsx`).** A rail row shows its icon. Pointed at or focused, the icon turns into a right chevron (a small button of its own) and a "..." appears at the right; the chevron folds the row's children open in place, indented 16px per level, each with its own icon, and turns down while open. Going and opening are separate acts: the label goes to the page, the chevron only folds. The row in use is lit; reaching a project (from a list, a link, a search result or a task) opens Projects and that project in the rail, so the page in use is always in view. The "..." has one real action, Open in new tab (no promise of anything else, 2.10).

  **What the children are, all real:** a section's views, which are what its pill offered (All requests and My requests; All agreements and My agreements; All nominations and My nominations; Users, Roles and Permissions; All species, Flora and Fauna; All notifications and each category; Home's My BioData and Flora and Fauna Dashboard). Under Projects, the projects, and under each project its views (Overview, Project records, Species, Artefacts and attachments, Edit details for a role that can edit). Picking a child opens that section or project straight at that view. A section with one view, and a section with none, has no chevron (a one-option switcher is dishonest).

  **A control for it.** The Prototype tools bar has a second control, "Rail", set to Nested by default and to Flat (the rail as it was, `?rail=flat`); it is independent of the Subtraction step. The view pill in the bar is unchanged, so a view can still be chosen there; whether the pill should go when the rail carries the views is the next subtraction and is the designer's call.

  **Open for the designer (CONTRACTS 0.4):** whether the pill should leave the bar now that the rail holds the views; Explore, Template Finder, Controlled Vocabulary, Reports and Voucher Management have no children (their facets and categories were not carried over); Notion lets a person drag rows to reorder or favourite them, which is not built; whether Projects should list only the projects someone has opened or favourited (all four are listed, which will not scale to the real count); whether the "..." should also offer Add to favourites once favourites show in the rail.
