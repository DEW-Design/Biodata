# 2026-11-10 - Ingestion page: a "?" on a concept shows its controlled vocabulary and definitions; SITETYPE kept

- **Nov 10 2026: the concept help pattern, on the ingestion engine page first.** The designer, with a mock of a record's Field table (a "?" after "Legacy Site ID" and a tooltip), commented "On hover: Control vocab + Definitions", and: "let's have this pattern on the ingestion engine page first before this is folded into prod." Job: a person reading an ingested record asks what a concept means and what it is read against. Principles: 4 Tell the truth (every word is the vocabulary's own), 1 Say less (an icon only where there is something to say), 5 Make it obvious, 3 One system (the design system's tooltip and the help icon the form Label draws). Closest pages: `components/base/input/label.tsx` (the help icon and tooltip), the Controlled Vocabulary (`cv-data.ts`).
  **Built.** `app/pages/data-ingestion-stress-test/concept-help.tsx` (`ConceptHelp`, `vocabularyFor`), used in the record's Field table (`page.tsx`). A concept is linked to the controlled vocabulary of the same name, never a near match ("Location Method & Desc" reads as "Location Method"). The tooltip's title is the vocabulary's name; its text is the vocabulary's own description, then up to six active values as "code - name" (a numbered vocabulary shows the name), "and N more", and "Archived" when its end date has passed. Composed from the design system's `Tooltip` and `TooltipTrigger` (no new component, 1.4): hover and keyboard focus both open it, the target is 40px (`hit-area`), Escape closes it. Inside the table, as in every react-aria grid, the help buttons are reached with the arrow keys, not Tab.
  **What gets one today.** Only a concept with a vocabulary of the same name: Datum, Location Method (the vocabulary is archived, its end date passed 30 Jun 2026, so the tooltip says so), IBRA Region and Occurrence Status. Every other concept, Legacy Site ID included, has no vocabulary and no definition anywhere in the repo, so it has no "?" (2.12) and nothing is written for it (0.3). The mock showed the icon on Legacy Site ID; it will appear there once a definition exists. Where the definitions should live is open (below).
  **SITETYPE kept** (designer: "Matters, keep"). It is no longer listed as "not used": it stays on a Site, Visit and species-derived record as a flagged field under its column name ("SITETYPE SU"), until the business names the concept.
  **Verified.** `tsc`, `eslint` on the page folder and `check:contracts` pass.
