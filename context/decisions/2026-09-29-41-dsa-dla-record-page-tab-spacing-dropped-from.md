- **Sept 29 2026: DSA/DLA record-page tab spacing dropped from 24px to 16px, and a real "Audit Log" tab added to both, per page feedback on `/pages/dsa/DSA-2026-01415?userRole=biodata-admin` ("Spacing between two tab items should decrease from 24px to 16px. Across all."; "Add 'Audit Log' for both DLA and DSA").**
  - **Spacing:** `TabList`'s own `underline` type (`components/application/tabs/tabs.tsx`) defaulted to `gap-3` (12px) regardless of size - every real record-page consumer (project-detail x3, the generated project page's own two tab rows, `data-overview.tsx`, DLA, DSA, nominations, User Management x2 - 10 call sites in total) had independently pasted a `className="gap-6"` override to get 24px, the exact "same role, different treatment" copy-paste drift CONTRACTS §1.9/§2.9 already exist to catch. Fixed once at the component: `underline`'s gap is now size-dependent (`md` → `gap-4`/16px, `sm` unchanged at `gap-3`/12px), and all 10 `gap-6` overrides were deleted so every one of them now inherits the corrected default instead of a locally-copied number. The two `size="sm"` underline tabs that were never part of this pattern (Home's "Filter by status", Explore's "Result type" - both plain filter switchers, not record sections) were left untouched, still 12px, since they were never at 24px and weren't named in the feedback.
  - **Audit Log:** DSA and DLA never tracked their own status history at all (unlike Nominations, which already has a real "Audit history" tab built from a `history: NominationEvent[]` array appended to on every transition). Built the identical mechanism for both, sharing one new type instead of three near-copies: `AgreementEvent` (`{status, at, by, note?}`) added to the already-shared `agreement-status.ts` (re-exported as `DsaEvent`/`DlaEvent`), plus a new `REVIEWING_ADMIN_NAME = "Jane"` constant in `agreement-scope.tsx` (the sanctioned BioData Admin placeholder persona, parallel to `CURRENT_USER_NAME`/"Olivia Wyatt") for every reviewer-side entry. `Dsa`/`Dla` both gained a real `history` field; `dsa-store.ts`/`dla-store.ts` each gained a `transition()` helper (mirroring `nomination-store.ts`'s own) that every status-changing function (`saveDsa`/`saveDla`, `startReview`, `hold`/`resume`, `approve`, `reject`, `cancel`) now routes through, so every future transition is recorded automatically, not just the seed data. All 14 DSA and 9 DLA seed records were backfilled with a plausible history chain (draft/submitted attributed to the real seeded requester for DLA, everything DSA-side attributed to "Jane" since DSA has no separate requester persona in this app - it's admin-managed end to end; the two seeded already-`closed` records get their final "closed" entry attributed to `"System"`, matching the exact convention the dataset-ingestion status trail already established for a transition nobody actually clicks a button for). The tab itself (`Tab id="audit" label="Audit Log"`) and its panel - a `RecordRow` per event, newest first, a status `Badge` plus who and an optional note - are a straight port of Nominations' own "Audit history" tab and its exact JSX shape, just relabelled to the literal wording asked for here (yet a third name for the same concept across three record types - `CONTEXT.md`'s own open item #11 on naming consistency already flags this; not resolved in this pass).
  - **Deliberately not logged as separate audit events:** the two automatic Approved-to-Active and Active-to-Closed transitions `effectiveStatus()` computes at read time rather than committing to the store - adding a synthetic entry for those would mean writing to the store from a pure read-time function, which is exactly what that function's own existing design comment says not to do ("computed at read time... rather than baked into the persisted state"). The two seeded `closed` DSA/DLA records get one explicit `"System"`-authored closing entry each since their seed data bakes the post-resolution status in directly; live records that cross from Approved to Active, or Active to Closed, purely by the calendar simply show their last real human decision (Approved) as the newest Audit Log entry, with no further row - a deliberate, honest simplification, not an oversight.
  - Verified `tsc --noEmit`/`eslint --max-warnings=0` clean on every touched file (`tabs.tsx`, both stores, both data files, both detail pages, `agreement-status.ts`, `agreement-scope.tsx`, and the 8 files that lost their `gap-6` override), `npm run check:contracts` clean, and live (a disposable Playwright install, discarded after, `package.json`/`package-lock.json` untouched): the exact reported screen (`DSA-2026-01415`) measured a real 16px gap between every tab and shows a real 4-entry Audit Log (Approved/Under review/Submitted/Draft, all "Jane", matching screenshot); the same 16px gap confirmed on DLA's "Request sections", Nominations' "Nomination sections", the generated project page's "Project views", and a real User Management user record's tabs; DLA's own Audit Log shows a mixed real trail (Olivia Wyatt's Draft/Submitted, Jane's Under review/Approved). Zero console errors throughout. Not committed.
