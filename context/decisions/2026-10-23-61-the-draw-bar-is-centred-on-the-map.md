# 2026-10-23 - The draw bar is centred on the map

- **Oct 23 2026: The draw bar is centred on the map.** The designer, with a screenshot while a polygon was being drawn: a red vertical line down the middle of the window, the note "why is this not centre aligned?" and an arrow to the "Drawing a polygon / Cancel" bar at the bottom: "fix."
  - **The fault:** the bar sat in the same row as the map key and was centred (`mx-auto`) in what was left of that row after the key, so its centre was about 230px right of the map's (1234 against 1037 in the screenshot's window): I had made it avoid the key by moving it, not by finding room for it.
  - **The fix:** the bar is centred on the map's own width, at every width. Where the map is 1500px or more wide it is absolutely centred on the key's baseline (the key reaches at most 456px from the left and the bar, about 440px wide, starts at least 108px beyond it at 1500px: measured gaps 308px at 2000, 151px at 1686, 108px at 1600); below that it goes under the key, centred, and never over it (2.8). Measured, bar centre minus map centre: **0px** at 2000, 1686, 1600, 1500, 1440, 1280 and 720px wide windows; no overlap with the key at any of them.
  - **"Centre" means the map, not the window.** The map is the area to the right of the rail (64px), so its centre is 32px right of the window's. The red line in the screenshot is at about the window's centre; a bar on the map's centre line reads as centred on the map, which is what it floats over. If the window's centre is wanted instead, it is a 32px shift and one class; say so.
  - **Checked live (Playwright, Super Admin; zero console errors):** the seven widths above, with a keyword search so the key is showing, looked at in a screenshot at 2000px. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the file and `npm run check:contracts` clean. The wording in the component comment now says "centred on the map" (it said "beside the key").
