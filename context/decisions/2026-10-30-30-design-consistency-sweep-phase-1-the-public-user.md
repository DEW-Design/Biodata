# 2026-10-30 - Design consistency sweep phase 1: the public user, 26 findings and 8 decisions

- **Oct 30 2026: the first of four phases of a design-system consistency sweep, for every page a signed-out (public) user can reach.** The designer: "let's run a consistency sweep in phases ... find all inconsistencies with the design system for all pages that fall under the public user. Flag what's wrong, what needs to be fixed, and the fix itself. Phase 2: Registered users. Phase 3: Biodata super admin. Phase 4: Biodata admin. Do only Phase 1 now. All behaviour mismatches should be noted as well." Run as the `design-consistency` skill. **Phases 2, 3 and 4 were not run.**
  - **What ran.** `tsc`, `check:contracts` and whole-repo `eslint` (the 27 errors and 9 warnings are in the Untitled UI ingest files and `lib/config-context.tsx`, none in a public page). A live Playwright sweep of 37 pages as `?userRole=public-user` (the nav screens, the project page's tabs, Explore, the observation page, the 10 auth pages, both marketing pages and 16 gated pages reached by URL): zero console errors and zero failed requests; every control's name and 40px target, every text's type and colour against the scale and the Figma set, 14 Tab stops per page, headings and landmarks, copy; and behaviour probes of every action a public user can press. Evidence (46 screenshots, the measurements and the scripts) is in `.context/audit/phase-1-public/`, gitignored.
  - **Result, in the audit file `audit/design-consistency-2026-10-30-phase-1-public-user.md`.** 26 findings, each with where, what is wrong, what needs fixing and the fix: 3 HIGH, 17 MED, 6 LOW. The visual layer holds (one typeface, one shell, text in Figma colours, a consistent sign-up dialog for every gated action). The findings are (1) what a signed-out visitor may see: a Draft and an Under review project are listed and the Draft opens; reviewer flags and a field note ("gate key held by Cleland ranger station") are readable; the Project access tab shows who has Full control; (2) how a restriction is shown: three patterns, and the 14 page-level gates promise "create one" and offer no Log in or Sign up; (3) the auth flow and marketing pages: the product named "Biodata SA", "BioData SA" and "What is Biodata", no `h1` or `main`, Title Case, and empty submits that say nothing; (4) single-component misses: the 17px wordmark, blue and red chips used as categories, "Go to record" outside its card on one tab, focus rings missing on the password toggle and the banner close, an unnamed icon button.
  - **Fixed on sight: nothing.** No colour primitive drifted (2.1c passes), and every finding is either a decision or touches more than a couple of files, which the skill reports and does not fix.
  - **Not changed by this sweep:** no source file, no contract, no baseline. `package.json` shows a `loading-dev` dependency; that is from the Badge loader work earlier today, not from the sweep, and the sweep's temporary scaffold was removed.
  - **Not covered.** Mobile, dark surfaces, screen reader output, touch, the project record panel and record page (phase 2, where records are editable), `/proto` and the Prototype tools bar.
  - **Open, for the designer (0.4), in the order that matters.** (1) Do signed-out visitors see Draft and Under review projects (recommend: published only). (2) Are flags and field notes public (recommend: members and reviewers). (3) Is the Project access tab public (recommend: remove it for them). (4) Completed's blue, the VIDEO chip and the pie chart's categorical colours (recommend: stay inside the Figma set, chart colours from a validated palette). (5) The header wordmark at 17px or 18px. (6) Which marketing layout survives. (7) Whether a stacked row's height is exempt from the 40px target. (8) Whether the stress-test page is public.
  - **Next.** Answers to 1 to 3 shape phases 2 to 4 (a registered user sees their own drafts, an admin sees all). Then the shared fixes that remove the most sites: one `RestrictedState` component, `SectionHeader as="h1"` with the auth shell's `h1` and `main`, per-section document titles, and the focus ring on the password toggle and banner close. Phase 2 on the designer's word.
