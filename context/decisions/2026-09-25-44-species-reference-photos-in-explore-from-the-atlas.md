# 2026-09-25 - species reference photos in Explore, from the Atlas of Living Australia (ALA), per designer direction ("build it now with an imageUrl field ... and the icon fallback. Use ALA if you can").

- **Sept 25 2026: species reference photos in Explore, from the Atlas of Living Australia (ALA), per designer direction ("build it now with an imageUrl field ... and the icon fallback. Use ALA if you can").** A species can be recognised by sight: the option 3 summary card leads with a photo (full width, 144px tall, with the credit under it: creator, licence, and a link to the image's ALA page), and the list cards for Species, Occurrences and Observations show a 40px thumbnail in place of the group icon. **Real images only (CONTRACTS 0.3):** for each of the 21 species in `search-data.ts`, ALA's own search gave the representative image for that exact scientific name, and its image metadata gave the creator and licence. **Kept:** CC BY and CC BY-NC (14 species, copies in `public/pages/species/`, about 900 KB in all, so the prototype does not depend on ALA being up). **Excluded on purpose:** images with no licence recorded (Macropus giganteus, Litoria raniformis, Acacia pycnantha) and CC BY-NC-ND (Emu: a cropped thumbnail is a derivative). **No ALA image under that name** (Macropus rufus, Neophema chrysogaster, Malurus cyaneus). Those species, and every non-species record, fall back to the group icon on a same-size tile, so nothing shifts or breaks. New page-shared files: `map-search/species-images.ts` (the map from scientific name to file, credit and licence; the one place to add or swap a photo), `map-search/species-photo.tsx` (`SpeciesPhoto`: fixed size, alt text is the common name, lazy, icon fallback); `ResultCard` gained an optional `thumb`, and `RecordPeekCard` shows the photo and credit. Verified live on option 3 (Belair National Park): thumbnails on 3 of 6 species cards, the others show their icon tile; the Echidna card opens with its photo and credit; zero console errors; `tsc`, `eslint`, `check:contracts` clean.
  - **For the designer to decide:** (1) **CC BY-NC (9 of the 14) covers non-commercial use only.** Whether a government service counts is a licensing call for DEW, not made here; swapping to DEW's own photo library is a one-file change in `species-images.ts`. (2) **A data problem the photos exposed:** the record "Western Grey Kangaroo" carries the scientific name Macropus giganteus, which is the Eastern Grey Kangaroo (the Western Grey is Macropus fuliginosus). ALA's image for that name is unlicensed so it is not shown, but the mismatch is in `search-data.ts` and was left alone (it changes a record). (3) Some ALA "representative" images are poor for identification (the Fairy Tern is a small bird in a large sky); ALA has others per species, and a curated choice would be better than the auto-picked one. (4) Not yet in the species table, the header search results or the record sidebar. Not committed.
