- **Sept 28 2026: floating panels on the Explore map never overlap, and that is now a rule (CONTRACTS.md §2.8), per designer feedback off a screenshot ("No overlaps like this. EVER.").** On option 2 the record summary card was positioned on its own (`top-[101px] right-4`, with a max height) and the "Species group" key on its own (`right-4 bottom-8`), so a tall card grew down over the key. Now one layer in `observations-search.tsx` lays out every floating panel: the search card on the left, and one right-hand column holding the zoom buttons, the summary card and the key, top to bottom. Panels in a flex layout cannot overlap; the card scrolls inside itself when short. Below 648px of map width the right column stacks under the search card instead. The bottom padding keeps the scale bar and the attribution clear. To do that the zoom buttons moved out of `SAMap` for this layout: new `map-zoom-buttons.tsx` (`MapZoomButtons`, a type-only Leaflet import so it is safe outside the `ssr: false` map bundle), with `SAMap`'s own `ZoomControls` now rendering the same component; option 2 passes `showZoomControls={false}` and `onMapReady`. **Summary card:** photo and details now scroll together and "Show in project" is pinned in a footer, because in a short window the square photo alone filled the card and pushed the button out of reach. **Search card title** no longer wraps to three lines when narrow (the header row wraps instead). **Sibling sweep:** option 1's search card spanned the full width below `lg`, under the zoom buttons, and at full height reached the scale bar; it now stops at `right-16` below `lg` and at `100% - 3.25rem`. The only other absolutely positioned map control, the Full screen button in `GeoExtentPicker`, sits top-left clear of the zoom buttons and was left alone. **§2.8** (added, a tightening, listed in the next audit): floating elements on the same surface must not cover each other at any size; lay them out in one layout, bound and scroll a growing panel, stack when there is no room; the floating dev tools (§3.8) are excepted because they are draggable. **Verified:** a Playwright pass measuring the boxes of the search card, zoom buttons, summary card, key, scale bar and attribution on option 2 at 1792x1120, 1440x900, 1280x720, 1024x700, 1024x560, 800x700 and 500x800 (no intersections), and option 1's card, zoom, scale and attribution at 1440x900, 1024x560, 800x560 and 500x700 (none); zero console errors; `tsc`, `eslint --max-warnings=0` on touched files, `npm run check:contracts` and the Pages build pass. **Open:** (1) the dev tools (role switcher, layout options, the Agentation toolbar) still sit bottom-right over the map chrome by default; they are draggable and §3.8 forbids compensating for them in product layout, so moving their default spot is a designer decision; (2) at phone widths the stacked layout is cramped (the app header itself does not fit there yet).
