# 2026-10-23 - An area and its shape answer the pointer: chip hover, cursor and press, shape hover and press, crosshair while drawing

- **Oct 23 2026: An area and its shape answer the pointer.** The designer, with a screenshot of the card and a heavier "POLYGON 1" on the map, while the report work was under way: "clicking on polygon one should show hover cursor states. /emil-design-vocabulary - please look at this. Please identify where this isn't working and fix, thank you!"
  - **The vocabulary (emil-design-vocabulary):** *Hover state*, "the visual change when a cursor moves over an interactive element. Should confirm interactivity through cursor change, color shift, or both. Color alone is not enough"; *Cursor*, "pointer means clickable. Default means static... The wrong cursor breaks the implicit contract"; *Active state*, "without it, buttons feel unresponsive"; *Affordance*, "when affordance is missing, users guess"; *Focus state*. The contract in the screenshot was broken both ways: the chip reacted on the map (the shape went heavier) and said nothing itself.
  - **Where it was not working (measured in a browser, before):**
    1. **The chip's label:** `cursor: default` (the design system's Tag is a static label), no colour shift when pointed at (white before and after), no press (`scale` none). Only its X had a pointer.
    2. **The shape on the map:** Leaflet gave it a pointer cursor, which says "press me", and pressing it did nothing, and pointing at it did not touch its chip: the link ran one way only.
    3. **While a shape was being drawn**, an existing shape still showed the pointer, not the crosshair, and a press on it was a point of the new shape under a cursor that said otherwise.
  - **The fix:**
    1. **Chip (the design system `Tag`, additive and opt-in):** a group with an `onAction` is *actionable*: `cursor: pointer`, the gray fill when pointed at (the same as every ghost's), and a press (`scale(0.96)`, on `data-pressed`, so pressing the X does not press the chip). Pressing it, or Enter on it from the keyboard, **takes the map to that area** (the fly-to the card already had for the rows: `fitRequest`, with the card's padding), so the pointer is true. A `highlighted` prop draws a tag as pointed at while its shape is.
    2. **Shape (`SAMap`, two new opt-in props `onBoundaryHover` and `onBoundaryClick`):** pointing at it lights its chip (the other chips stay white) and outlines it; pressing it takes the map to it (zoom 7 to 8, measured).
    3. **Drawing:** the shapes ignore the pointer while a tool is active, and the container is marked `is-drawing` from `DrawBridge` (a React `className` on the Leaflet container is applied once, so it is added by hand), with one rule in `globals.css`: the crosshair over everything, the shapes already drawn too. Measured: the cursor over an existing shape is `crosshair` while drawing and `pointer` otherwise; a press on it while drawing does not move the map.
  - **A decision inside it, to confirm:** "for now, remove the controls" had taken click-to-zoom off the areas (`2026-10-23-54`). A pointer and a fill on a chip that did nothing would be a promise with nothing behind it (2.10), so the press is the one thing that came back, and only as the answer to the press: no eye, no menu, no count. If a chip should only light up and not move the map, take `onAction` off the group and the pointer and fill go with it.
  - **Checked live (Playwright, Super Admin; zero console errors):** a drawn polygon and an entered point as two chips. Label hovered: cursor pointer, background `rgb(255, 255, 255)` to `rgb(248, 248, 247)`, the polygon's stroke 2 to 4; pressed: scale 0.96; pressing the point's chip then the polygon's moves the map between zoom 10 and zoom 8 with the polygon at 800px wide; Enter on a focused chip does the same; pointing at the polygon on the map lights its chip and not the other; pressing the polygon from a zoomed-out view takes the map to it. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the touched files and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
  - **Not checked:** touch (there is no hover; the press works), Safari, a screen reader on the chip as an action (react-aria gives it the row role with an action; its X is still labelled "Remove this tag" on every chip).
