# 2026-10-23 - Design principles written down, craft documented, and explorations start design-first (contract 0.10)

- **Oct 23 2026: Design principles written down, craft documented, and explorations start design-first (contract 0.10).** The designer: "all the amazing work we've been doing to make the design of the interface nice and elegant, let's make sure it is documented too. Also, do we have underlying design principles? I feel like that work trailed off. Let's start to approach explorations with design-first principles." Nothing on any screen was changed; this is documentation, a reference for sessions, and one conduct clause.
  - **The honest answer to "do we have principles?":** not as a document. They existed in three scattered places: the cognitive-load list in `ref-shell.md` (written 29 Sept; the one hand-kept statement of "why"), the cognitive-load audit of the project page (30 Sept, `context/reports/`, which judges the page against that list), and, implicitly, in the contracts, which say what to do and what not to, each with the incident behind it, but not what they protect. After 30 Sept the work went into contracts, patterns and polish (more than 550 dated decisions, about 70 clauses) and the list was never revisited, which is the trailing off the designer felt.
  - **How the principles were found, and how far to trust them:** read back out of the decision log, not invented. The designer's own words in the 2026 decision files were extracted by theme (consistency, purpose and noise, clarity and affordance, hierarchy and type, polish, truth of data, cognitive load, touch, motion), the same few things came up in all of them, and they were grouped into nine. Every quotation on the page was checked against the file it cites (a few entries are the log's words about what the designer asked and are shown without quotation marks). The wording of each principle, its "ask of a screen" and its "held by" clauses are the agent's. **It is a draft for the designer to confirm.** The method is keyword extraction over the decision files and the saved feedback, in one pass, so a principle with a thin trail (7, "design every state", and 8, "reach everyone", have one or two quotes each) is thinner evidence than the rest.
  - **The nine:** 1 Say less (nothing on screen without a purpose); 2 One thing first (one focal point, a type ladder, hierarchy by subtraction); 3 One system (the same job, the same pattern; borrow, extend once); 4 Tell the truth (real components, content and data; a view shows its source's words); 5 Make it obvious (the designer's standing test: "I know where I've come from, I know what to do here, and I know how to get back", from `2026-10-23-09`); 6 Reveal in steps (tier, take the click, a fade says there is more); 7 Design every state (and every role); 8 Reach everyone (finger, mouse, keyboard, contrast, no dependence on motion); 9 Craft shows (the details are made and measured, not eyeballed). Four pairs that pull against each other, with the resolution the work so far has used for each, are listed so a case that falls between two is decided the same way.
  - **Built:**
    - **`.claude/rules/ref-principles.md`** (hand-maintained, loads when a screen, a lab, a component, a doc page or the tools bar is read; indexed in `CONTEXT.md`): the nine in short, each with the question to ask, the pairs that pull apart, and the order an exploration goes in.
    - **Design principles, `/principles`:** the nine in full, with the designer's words and their dates and decision ids, the question to ask, the clauses that hold each, a link to a screen or pattern that shows it, the pairs that pull apart, the exploring order, a worked example (Map visualise, then the Map list card), and what the page is not (not a replacement for the contracts; not finished; not a style guide).
    - **Craft details, `/principles/craft`:** the polish catalogue with its values and where each lives: the alpha edge (shown on three backgrounds against a solid grey), concentric corners (three pairs on the scale and one pinched), lift as a shadow and separation as a line, the type ladder (a real result row beside the treatment of each line), space on the 4px grid and the card's 16px, scroll with no bar and the fade, quiet motion and the ghost button (live), the hit area (drawn: `data-show-hit-area`, a docs-only rule in `globals.css` that outlines every hit area inside it), the source's words, and how a detail is checked. Each entry names the clause and the decision with the measurements.
    - **Nav, README and `lib/nav.ts`:** a new first section, Principles, with the two pages; the README's "three sections" is four; CONTRACTS 3.9's note on the sections that are not A-Z names it.
    - **CONTRACTS 0.10 "Explore design-first"** (core, so it loads every session; `REVIEW`): an exploration starts from the principles: name the job and the two or three principles that bear on it and where they pull apart (ask one precise question where nothing settles it); stand on the ground (states and roles, the closest page, the content that really exists, the components that exist); make options as real-component labs where the direction is open, each saying which principle it favours; judge against the principles and the designer's standing tests before showing it, saying what was measured; record why in the principle's terms, and add the details to Craft details. A principle never overrides a clause (0.1).
    - **Memory:** the working agreement is saved, so a session that starts without this conversation still does it.
  - **Checked:** both pages load (HTTP 200, no console errors), the sidebar lists Principles first, and the drawn hit areas on the Craft page measure 106 by 40, 40 by 40 (three) and 57 and 60 by 40, the sizes the contract names; the home page's section counts still render. The two hyphens of `--hit-min` render as a dash in the page's font, so those paragraphs switch ligatures off (2.3: no em-dashes). `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the new pages and `npm run check:contracts` clean.   - **Open, for the designer:**
    1. **Confirm, rename, merge or add.** Is it these nine? Candidates not taken as separate principles: "measure, don't eyeball" (kept inside Craft shows), "roles reduce features, not screens" (inside Design every state), and "explore with options" (in the exploring order). If a principle is wrong, change it in `ref-principles.md` and `/principles` together.
    2. **Is 0.10 the right weight?** It binds every exploration to a six-step order; if that is too heavy for a small change, it can be scoped to new screens and layout options, or kept as the reference only.
    3. **The four resolutions** (Say less against Make it obvious, One system against Craft shows, Reveal in steps against three clicks, Tell the truth against Say less) are what the work has done, not decisions the designer has made; they should be confirmed or replaced.
    4. **Where Principles sits:** first in the docs nav, ahead of Primitives. It could be last, or under Patterns.
    5. **What was left out of Craft details:** colour (the Primitives page has it), the radius and spacing scales themselves (Primitives), and the shell's contracts (3.x), because they are rules and already documented; and any detail of the screens other than the map cards and the shared components, which were not catalogued one by one. It grows as details are made, by 0.10 step 5.
