# 2026-10-23 - Explore parks form: no Done, no title, it leaves like a popover

- **Oct 23 2026: Explore's "Choose national parks" step loses its Done and its title, and goes away the way a popover does.** The designer, with two screenshots of the step open under the card header (two parks chosen, two park chips under "Search results", Done at the right of the title): "also for this - 'done' looks so weird there - it has absolutely no use / value being there. Remove." and, of a crop of the same step, the search field, the results heading and six chips: "this entire bit looks a bit odd and bloated".
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 1 Say less (a button that only closes a thing that has nothing to confirm; a title that says what the field under it already says), 5 Make it obvious (a way out must still exist), 3 One system (1.9: "selection applies live ... closing keeps what was chosen, there is no hidden Apply"). The first two pull a little apart: removing Done must not leave a form with no way out.
  - **Why Done had no value:** each park is a chip on the map the moment it is ticked, so the button confirmed nothing; it only closed the step. Coordinates and a shapefile are different: they are transactions (values are entered, then added or uploaded), so their Cancel is a real choice and stays.
  - **The change:**
    1. **No Done, and no title for the parks step.** Its title ("Choose national parks") repeated the menu item just pressed and the field's own label ("Parks"). The step is now the two labelled fields, 88px high (it was 120px).
    2. **It leaves the way a popover does ("light dismiss"):** a press anywhere outside it, or Escape, closes it; the parks stay as chips. The dropdown's list is portaled out of the step, so a press in the list, or in the radius field, does not close it. Escape on the open list closes the list only (1.9 item 1); a second Escape closes the step. Escape also closes the coordinates and shapefile steps, as their Cancel does.
    3. **The search field is 12px under the step** (it was 4px: the step's rule and the field's border read as touching).
  - **Kept as it was:** coordinates and shapefile steps keep a title and a Cancel; chips, the results heading and the list are untouched.
  - **Checked live (Playwright, Super Admin; zero console errors):** opened the step (88px, "Parks / Choose one or more parks / Radius (km)", no Done); opened the dropdown (17 parks), ticked two (the step stayed open, two chips); Escape closed only the list; a press in the radius field kept the step; a press on the map closed it and left both chips; Escape in the radius field closed it; the coordinates step stayed open after a press outside and closed on Cancel. Gap from the step to the search field 12px in all three steps. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the file and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
  - **Not checked:** touch, Safari, a screen reader for the step closing on an outside press (focus is not moved when it closes).
  - **Open, for the designer (the second sentence was not fully pinned down, so only the step was touched):** "this entire bit ... odd and bloated" was read as the step. If it also meant the chips and the results heading, the candidates are: the chips (six uppercase 12px chips on three rows, each park already named in the field as "3 selected"), the "Search results 61" heading that counts across kinds while the tabs below count 14 and 5, and the Hide list control. Say which, and say if the title should come back on the parks step for orientation. Also open: whether light dismiss should also apply to the coordinates and shapefile steps (they do not, because a half-typed coordinate would be lost by a press on the map, which is where a person looks for one).
