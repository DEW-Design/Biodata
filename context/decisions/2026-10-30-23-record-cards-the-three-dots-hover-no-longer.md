# 2026-10-30 - Record cards: the three-dots hover no longer hides the dots

- **Oct 30 2026: the "..." button on a record's gradient card keeps its dots visible when hovered, pressed or focused.** The designer, with a close-up of it hovered (a near-white square with a faint ring, and the dots gone): "what's happened to this hover state? three dots on the project gradient card."
  - **What happened.** Nothing changed this week: the hover was wrong from the start and had not been looked at. `Dropdown.DotsButton` is a light-surface button whose hover fill is `bg-primary_hover` (gray-50, near white). The two gradient cards drew it with white dots (`text-white/80 hover:text-white`) and left that fill on, so on hover the dots turned white on a near-white square, and the ring (the brand focus colour) was lost on the dark teal.
  - **What changed.** `Dropdown.DotsButton` takes an opt-in `onDark` (`components/base/dropdown/dropdown.tsx`, 1.6; every other use is unchanged): white dots at 80%, a white wash at 15% on hover and press with the dots at full white, and a white focus ring (2px, offset 2px). The two gradient cards use it instead of overriding the light button's classes at the call site: the record cards (`RecordActionBar onDark`: DLA, DSA, nominations, users, roles, reports, vouchers ...) and the project card (`ProjectCardActions`). The Dropdown doc page describes it.
  - **Looked for others like it.** The only other buttons on the gradient card are white buttons (Upload, the upload chip, a record's next step) with their own states; the other dots buttons (a table row, a report's actions, a card's menu) are on light surfaces and are as they were.
  - **Checked.** `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the three files and `npm run check:contracts` pass. Live (Playwright, zero console errors), computed styles: on the project card and on a DLA record's card, at rest the dots are white at 80% on no fill; hovered, the fill is white at 15% and the dots full white; focused by keyboard, a 2px white ring 2px out. On a light surface (a report's row) the hover is still the gray-50 fill with the darker dots and the ring is still the brand colour.
  - **Not checked.** The pressed state by eye (the same classes as hover); touch.
  - **Open, for the designer (0.4).** The wash is 15% white; if it should read stronger or fainter against the gradient it is one class (`bg-white/15`).
