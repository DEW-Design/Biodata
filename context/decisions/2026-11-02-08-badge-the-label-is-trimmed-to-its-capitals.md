# 2026-11-02 - Badge: the label is trimmed to its capitals so it sits in the middle of the pill

- **Nov 2 2026: Text inside a pill has its vertical trim removed.** The designer, with a screen recording of the Badge doc page (pill-color, brand, md) measured with a ruler: the capitals sat further from the top of the pill than from the bottom, and "this is the only way to fix this". Cause: a line box is taller than the letters in it, and Barlow's room above its capitals (0.3em) is not its room below the baseline (0.2em), so a label centred by its line box sits low. Fix, once, in the component: the new `pill-text` utility (`app/globals.css`) wraps each Badge label (`Badge`, `BadgeWithDot`, `BadgeWithIcon`, `BadgeWithFlag`, `BadgeWithImage`, `BadgeWithButton`, and the number in `CountBadge`) and sets `text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic` with `padding-block: calc((1lh - 1cap) / 2)`, which cuts the line box to the capitals and gives back the same half-difference on each side, so every pill keeps the height it had (30px at md measured 29.98 before and after; sm 24, lg 32). Both lines are inside `@supports (text-box: ...)`, so a browser that cannot trim keeps the pill as it was. A label that truncates inside a capped pill still does (`min-width: 0`, and a `.truncate` child is a block): checked on the Report parameters and Reports landing cases. Clause 1.6 (an opt-in prop) is overridden by the designer's instruction for this change: it is on for every badge, as the hit area was (2.16), because the instruction was that the text in a pill is trimmed.
- **Measured** (Chromium, the Badge doc page, ink of the capitals against the pill's edges, top gap/bottom gap in device px): 14px md 40/39 before and after at 4x; 12px sm 84/71 before and 76/79 after at 8x (1.6px low became 0.4px high); 16px lg 85/79 before and 77/87 after at 8x (0.75px low became 1.25px high). At page zoom 200% on a 2x display md went 42/37 to 38/41. So the trim centres the capitals in the layout, but the browser then snaps the baseline to a whole pixel, which leaves up to about 0.6px of offset in either direction, and it is not always smaller than before: lg is a little worse, sm much better, md the same at 100% and better when zoomed. I could not reproduce the designer's 18/16 reading on md at 100%.
- **Open:** other pills that centre text by its line box (`Tag`, the filter chips, the table's status cells that draw their own pill, `rounded-full` spans in screens) were not changed; a grep and the same measurement are due before the clause is promoted. An alternative with the same aim and no baseline snapping problem in other browsers is `ascent-override` and `descent-override` on the Barlow `@font-face`, which would centre the capitals in every line box in the app, not only pills; it is a bigger change and is not made. Not committed.
