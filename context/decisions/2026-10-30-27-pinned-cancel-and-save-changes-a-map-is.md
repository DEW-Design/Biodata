# 2026-10-30 - Pinned Cancel and Save changes: a map is its own layer, so it never paints over the bar

- **Oct 30 2026: the pinned Cancel and Save changes bar of an editing card is solid over the map, and so is everything on the map.** The designer, with two screenshots of the Geographic extent card in edit mode, scrolled so the map ran under the bar: the map's tiles and the drawn circle were painted over the bar's white background (only the Save and Cancel buttons were clear, at the right of the map), and then the picker's "Full screen" button drawn on top of the bar: "edge case to be dealt with." and "and this."
  - **Cause, measured.** The bar's slot is `z-10`. Leaflet gives its panes and controls z-index values from 400 to 1000, and the picker's own "Full screen" button is `z-[1001]`. Neither the map nor the box that holds the button was a stacking context, so their z-index values competed with the page's and won. Reproduced in a 700px window with the map under the bar: `elementFromPoint` at the bar's centre returned the map's SVG circle, not the bar (and the first check of `2026-10-30-24` missed it because it probed only the Save button, which sits clear of the map).
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 5 Make it obvious (the bar is a control and reads as one, solid), 9 Craft shows (a layer is above or below the whole map, never between its parts), 3 One system (every map, once). They agree.
  - **What changed.** (1) `app/globals.css`: every map, `.leaflet-container`, is `isolation: isolate`, next to the rules that already set the map's font and text tokens. Everything inside a map now competes only inside it. (2) `geo-extent-picker.tsx`: the box that holds the inline map and its "Full screen" button is `isolate`, as `ExpandableMap`'s box already was, so the button's `z-[1001]` stays inside it. (3) `lib/layers.ts` says so beside the z-index levels, so the next overlay does not guess. Nothing was given a higher z-index.
  - **Looked for others.** The other elements at `z-[1000]` or above that sit near a map are Explore's and the dataset map's full-page overlay panels (inside the page's own layer, with no pinned bar over them) and the `ExpandableMap` Expand button (already inside its isolated box). None is under a pinned bar.
  - **Checked.** `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the two touched files and `npm run check:contracts` pass. Live (Playwright, 700px window, Adelaide Hills as BioData Admin, zero console errors): with the map's top under the bar, the element on top at the bar's centre, at the map's x, is the bar (before: the map's SVG), and the "Full screen" button and the zoom-in button, both under the bar's top edge, have the bar on top of them. The bar's solid white in a screenshot. Regression: Explore's zoom-in and search card, the expanded map dialog's zoom-in (inside the dialog) and Add Project's zoom-in are each the element on top at their centres.
  - **Not checked.** The other maps in a modal (a location restriction, the nomination form, DLA's Add a location) by eye; a touch screen.
  - **Open, for the designer (0.4).** None.
