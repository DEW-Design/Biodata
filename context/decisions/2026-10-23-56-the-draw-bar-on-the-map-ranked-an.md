# 2026-10-23 - The draw bar on the map, ranked: an icon tile, a title, a hint, a quiet Cancel

- **Oct 23 2026: The draw bar on the map, ranked: an icon tile, a title, a hint, a quiet Cancel.** The designer, with a crop of the bar ("Drawing a circle  Click and drag on the map to set the centre and radius.  [X Cancel]") and a reference of a card that ranks its parts (a small grey label, a large price, a quiet description, one white button, two quiet links): "can we please make this polished? /emil-ui-polish let's bring in some hierarchy on this."
  - **The fault:** the bar was flat. The title and its instruction ran on one line at one size, told apart only by weight, and Cancel was a secondary button (white, a ring) that weighed as much as the words, so nothing came first (Principle 2, One thing first).
  - **The order now (`DrawStatusBar`, `observations-search.tsx`):** (1) *what is happening*: the title "Drawing a circle" or "Drawing a polygon", 14/600 primary, with an icon tile before it, the tool's own icon (circle or pentagon, the icons the chips and the Add area menu use) on a brand-50 tile, the colour the shape is drawn in on the map, so the bar and the shape read as one; (2) *how to do it*: under the title, 12/400 tertiary, the supporting-line layer of a result row; (3) *the way out*: Cancel, last and quietest, as the design system's **Tertiary** button (no fill until pointed at, then the gray fill), where it was Secondary. The title and the hint are 20px and 16px lines, 36px together, the height of the tile and of the Cancel button, so the row has one height (52px inside a 1px edge) and an even 8px of room all round (measured: 9, 9, 9 and 9px with the border).
  - **Type, with its sources (2.9):** title 14/600 `rgb(46, 41, 37)` (text-primary), the layer the bar already had and the buttons' own 14; hint 12/400 `rgb(112, 107, 104)` (text-tertiary), the supporting lines of `ResultCard`. Measured with `getComputedStyle`.
  - **Surfaces (2.13):** the bar's 16px corner, less the 8px between its edge and the tile and the button, is the 8px each of them has (measured: bar 16, tile 8). Depth is the card shadow and the alpha edge every map surface shares, not a border.
  - **/emil-ui-polish pass, measured in a browser:** body font smoothing is antialiased; Cancel's weight is 600 at rest and on hover (no reflow); its hover transition is a named list of properties over 100ms, never `all`; its press is `scale(0.96)`; its hit area is 94 by 40px around a 94 by 36px button; the focus ring is a 2px solid grey-blue (neutral), kept; the decorative tile is `pointer-events: none`, `user-select: none` and `aria-hidden`; the entrance is an `enter` animation of 200ms with an ease-out curve (opacity and a 8px rise, transform only, behind `motion-safe`); nothing in the bar moves layout, and its width does not change while a shape is drawn. The bar's own computed `transition-property` reads `all`, which is the browser's default with a 0s duration; no transition is authored on it.
  - **Not done, on purpose:** no exit animation (the bar is unmounted the moment drawing ends, so it goes at once; an exit would need presence handling for a half-second the person does not wait through), and no "Esc" key hint beside Cancel (Escape does cancel, so the hint would be true, but it would be a fourth thing on a bar that is meant to be quiet; say if you want it).
  - **Checked live (Playwright, Super Admin; zero console errors):** the circle and polygon bars at 1686px and 720px (the bar stacks under the key below 1000px); Cancel hovered shows the gray fill; the press-and-release cancels the drawing. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the file and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
