# 2026-10-25 - Species tab cards: a restricted species' card has the same rhythm as every other

- **Oct 25 2026: On the project's Species tab (Cards view), the card of a restricted species lines up with every other species card.** The designer, by page feedback on `/proto/post-6-oct` (BioData Super Admin, 1694x994), on a card in `SpeciesView`: "This card looks inconsistent with the species card we have on the species tab. Let's make the design of the card consistent with that." (refined with `/refine-prompt`: the table and the prompt were shown with the reply).
  - **Reading, to confirm:** "this card" is the card that differs from the others on the same tab, and "the species card on the species tab" is the others. It was found by measuring, not by the click (the click names a card element, not which). The other reading, that the project's species cards should look like Explore's Species tab rows, would be a larger change and was not made; see Open.
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 3 One system (a control keeps its treatment across states; here the "Restricted" state), 7 Design every state, 9 Craft shows (measured, not eyeballed). They agree; none pulls against another.
  - **The fault, measured (7 cards, 3 columns at 1694px):** six cards had the title line at 20px and every line under it at the same offsets from the card's top (name 17, scientific name 39, family and group 61, the Records, Sites and Last recorded rows 89, "Go to record" 169; card 206px). The card with the "Restricted" Badge had its title row 24px, so everything under it sat 4px lower (43, 65, 93, 173) and the card was taller, out of step with the cards beside it. The Badge (small, 24px high) is taller than the 20px line of the name it sits beside. At two columns (about 1100px) the badge also wrapped onto a second line and the card was 28px taller still.
  - **The change (`app/pages/project-detail/species-view.tsx`, the Cards view only; production and the lab both use it):**
    1. The badge's box counts as 20px in the title row (`-my-0.5` takes back the 2px above and below), so it no longer sets the row's height. It is still the design system's Badge, the same size, colour and place, beside the name.
    2. The name and the badge are one line that does not wrap, as on Explore's result card (`ResultCard`): the name is cut after its last whole word that fits (the existing `FittedText`, with the whole name as its tooltip) and the badge is `shrink-0`. This is borrowed (0.9), not built a second time (1.10).
  - **Measured after (Playwright, Super Admin):** all 7 cards have the same offsets (17 / 39 / 61 / 89 / 169) and the same height (206px) at 1694px in the lab, at 1100px in the lab (two columns; before, a second set of offsets and a card of 234px), and on `/pages/project-detail`. Zero console errors. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the file and `npm run check:contracts` clean.
  - **Sibling grep (0.6 item 5):** the one other place that puts a Restricted Badge on a species name is the Table view's Access column (its own cell, no title line), unchanged; Explore's result card takes a `badge` prop but no screen passes this one. No other card puts the 24px badge on a 20px name line.
  - **No new type layer (2.9):** the name keeps `text-sm font-semibold text-primary` (the card's own classes, now on `FittedText`); the badge, the other lines and the rows are untouched.
  - **Not checked:** Registered User and BioData Admin (the card has nothing role-dependent), Safari, touch. A species whose scientific name or family line is long still wraps to two lines (only the name was made single-line), so such a card is taller by design.
  - **Open, for the designer:** whether "the species card we have on the species tab" meant Explore's Species tab, whose rows (a photo, the name in 14/500, the scientific name in italic, one meta line, no border) are the redesigned card of today. If so, the project's cards (a bordered card with a Records, Sites and Last recorded list and a "Go to record" link) would be rebuilt to that language, and what happens to the three rows and the link is the question: they are what this tab is for.
