# 2026-10-30 - Project people: a lab of four ways to draw a person, from the name leading to a contact card each

- **Oct 30 2026: a lab, `/proto/project-people`, compares four ways to draw a person on the project Overview's Published by and Project managers cards. The product page is unchanged.** The designer, with a screenshot of the Project managers card (hovering the first manager): "the hierarchy of reading is such a mess too - i mean, where do i start reading? What if we could maybe look into contact cards? What if that works out better?" It was run through `/refine-prompt` first (the table and the refined prompt are in the conversation). "What if" is a question, so this is options and not a replacement (0.10, 4.4).
  - **The job.** Someone reading who runs a project, on the Overview, as any role. **Principles at stake (0.10):** 2 One thing first (a focal point per person), 3 One system (the person the design system already draws; the card the DSA, DLA and user pages already use), 1 Say less (a rule, an avatar or a card per person is a mark: does it earn its place), 7 Design every state. Where they pull apart: 2 and 3 want a stronger person; 1 asks what each added mark tells you.
  - **What was wrong, measured.** Name (14px, 500), role (14px, 400, beside the name), team (14px, 400) and the email and phone (14px, 400) in two colours: one size, a 100 weight step, so the eye has no entry (the first change this evening, `2026-10-30-26`, only took the team line from 12px to 14px and added a rule; it did not give the block a first line).
  - **The four options, on the real Overview** (`ProjectTab`, the real cards, the Adelaide Hills seed, `peopleStyle` opt-in on `ProjectTab`, default "rows", so nothing changes until one is chosen, 1.6). The Prototype tools bar has the layout option and a second control, People: two managers, one, none named.
    - **A, as it is.** Kept as the baseline.
    - **B, the name leads.** The name is the one semibold line (14px, 600, the weight the design system's own person title and the card titles use, no new layer, 2.9), the Primary badge after it, role and team as one line under it ("Research, DEW Biodiversity Team", a comma between attributes, 2.3), then the contacts. Favours 2. No new mark.
    - **C, an avatar leads.** The design system's `AvatarLabelGroup` (an avatar, a semibold name, one supporting line), the contacts under the text. Favours 3. The avatar is the person's initials, which repeat the name: 2.12 removes a glyph that repeats its label, and there is no photo in the data.
    - **D, a card per person.** C inside a card of its own (12px, 20px of padding, the same chrome as the page's other cards), the Published by and Project managers cards dissolved into their title, edit icon and "Details needed" badge, with the cards under them; the full card comes back when nobody is named, so the empty state keeps its container. Favours 3 (a card as DSA and DLA draw a contact). It costs the card per section, which the designer asked to keep ("the shell for project details has to be consistent"), and loses the warning border on a section that needs details (the badge stays).
