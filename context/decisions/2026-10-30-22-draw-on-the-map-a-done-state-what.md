# 2026-10-30 - Draw on the Map: a done state, what was drawn and a way to remove it

- **Oct 30 2026: the picker's "Draw on the Map" tab says what was drawn and offers to remove it.** The designer, with a screenshot of a Geographic extent card with a polygon drawn on its map: "no done state?" Right: before a shape, the tab read "Draw a polygon or circle on the map to define this area" over two tool buttons; after one, it read exactly the same, and only the shape on the map said it had worked. The other three ways of defining the area each show what was chosen (the list's value, the coordinates, the shapefile's name); the map alone showed nothing.
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 7 Design every state (nothing yet, drawing, done), 5 Make it obvious (that it worked, what it is, how to undo it), 3 One system (the three other tabs have a filled state). They agree.
  - **What changed (`geo-extent-picker.tsx`).** The hint line above the drawing tools is a status line of one height with two states. With nothing drawn it is the hint, as before. With a shape it is a green check, what it is, and a "Remove" button: "Polygon, 4 points", "Circle, 65.6 km radius", or for a circle of a named place "15 km radius around Coorong National Park" (the sample content's). Remove empties the area (and Continue is blocked again, as for any empty extent). The line is `role="status"`, so it is announced when a shape lands. Drawing again replaces the shape, so the line does not explain that.
  - **Where, and why not under the map.** First put under the map, the line fell at the fold of a 1030px window, under the Prototype bar; above the tools it is always in view. Both states are 36px tall, the height of the button, so the tools and the map below do not move between them (measured: 318px from the top of the page in the idle, drawn and removed states).
  - **Who has it.** The picker is one component, so every place that draws gets it: the project page's Geographic extent edit card (the screenshot), Add Project (option 1 and option 2), a location restriction, the nomination form's location. Only a shape drawn on the map shows it; a place chosen from the list, coordinates or a shapefile are shown by their own tabs.
  - **Checked.** `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the file and `npm run check:contracts` pass. Live (Playwright, zero console errors): Add Project (the Expand variant) and the draft project's edit card (the Full screen variant, the screenshot's) show the line before and after drawing a polygon; Remove brings back the hint and blocks Continue; a circle drawn by hand reads "Circle, 65.6 km radius"; the pre-filled sample reads "15 km radius around Coorong National Park"; the tools stay at the same height in all states.
  - **Not checked.** The nomination form and a location restriction (same component, not opened); a screen reader's announcement; a polygon with many points (the line shows only the count).
  - **Not done, and open for the designer (0.4).** (1) **While drawing a polygon there is still no Done, Undo or Cancel.** A polygon is closed by clicking its first point (the cursor's tooltip says so); the tool button reads "Drawing…" and clicking it again cancels. Buttons for those would need the map to expose leaflet-draw's handler (`completeShape`, `deleteLastVertex`). (2) **The card's "Details needed" badge stays until Save**, after a shape is drawn, because it reads what is saved, not what is being edited. (3) The line could carry the polygon's area in square kilometres; it is not computed anywhere today.
