# 2026-10-12 - Post 6 Oct lab: the Linear-style shell at admin scale

- **Oct 12 2026: Post 6 Oct lab: the Linear-style shell at admin scale.** The designer (same Figma lo-fi, node 435:62): "Let's explore for admins - we'll take the largest UI scope and work it down." Still strictly a prototype: `/proto/post-6-oct`, nothing folded into the product. Follows `2026-10-12-01-post-6-oct-lab-a-linear-style-shell.md`.

  **Reading of the brief:** the largest scope is the BioData Super Admin (13 rail sections, 8 Add items, three review queues). The lab now opens as that role when the address names none, and the Prototype tools bar "Viewing as" works it down to BioData Admin (11 sections), Registered User (7) and Public user (3). The role is the Super Admin's until the designer says the largest scope should be something else.

  **What was built.** Every rail section opens as a tab; a table row or button that opens a project or a section opens a tab too (a react-aria `RouterProvider` over the panel). Sections with a clean list are the real lists without their shells: Projects, DLA, DSA, Nominations, User Management (Users, Roles, Permissions), Taxonomy, Notification Management, Controlled Vocabulary and Template Finder. Home, Explore, Voucher Management and Reports show a marked gap (`<Gap>`) and a link to the real page. Record pages are not in the lab: a row that opens one leaves for the real page with the role kept.

  **Column 2 has no home in this layout, so it folds into the bar:** the view menu (the pill) carries what column 2 listed as places (All requests and My requests, All agreements and My agreements, All nominations and My nominations, Users and Roles and Permissions, All species and Flora and Fauna, All projects and My projects, a notification category); the "..." menu carries the Actions group (Export CSV, wired for DLA and DSA only). A role with one view has no pill (a one-option switcher is dishonest). Template Finder's facets and Controlled Vocabulary's categories were not carried over.

  **Attention, by role.** The rail card shows the first and the bell lists all: a Registered User's three Home tasks; a reviewer's queues, counted by the same rule as each list's own banner so the card and the page never give two numbers (DLA requests under review, nominations submitted and waiting for the panel) plus flagged concepts open across the projects. A task opens the section tab with its status filter (DLA: under review; nominations: submitted) or, for flagged concepts, the real page.

  **Findings at scale.** (1) Tabs: 13 sections plus projects overflow the strip, so past six tabs the ones not in use collapse to their icon (a project shows its code, since every project is a folder); the name is the tooltip. (2) The rail: 13 sections, the card, the logo and the legal links fit at 1024px high; at 720px the card's detail line is dropped and the section list still scrolls. (3) A list's own heading repeats the tab and the crumb (Data Licencing Agreements, three times on screen). (4) The DLA, DSA and Users lists use the full panel height with their pagination pinned, which reads better than the current shell's narrower main.

  **Open for the designer (CONTRACTS 0.4):** whether every section should be a tab at all, or only records (a project) should, with a section replacing in place; whether Home, Explore, Voucher Management and Reports come next; where Template Finder's facets and a status filter go now (the view menu holds one choice, not a facet group); whether the crumb or the list's heading should go, as both say the section; the same shell for record pages and forms (a form's sections were column 2); which of the smaller roles to work down first.
