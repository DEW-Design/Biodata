# 2026-10-25 - Home patterns from Mobbin, by what each role is asking: proposed, nothing built

- **Oct 25 2026: Mobbin searched for Home and dashboard patterns that fit each role's mental model; a list is proposed and the work stops there (CONTRACTS 2.6).** The designer, after the Home options of `2026-10-25-03`: "look at /mobbin for patterns. This has to be a pattern that aligns to the mental models of users. You also have enough context of each user." (refined with `/refine-prompt`). No application code was changed.
  - **The workflow (2.6):** ingest, extract patterns, **propose a list and stop**, map to our components, build, QA, document. This entry is the first three. A reference gives interaction and IA, never colour, spacing, radius, type or components, so every pattern below is stated as what a screen puts first, hides and moves a person on to.
  - **What each role is asking (from `ref-roles.md`, `ref-shell.md` and the 5 and 25 Oct Home audits, not from Mobbin):**
    1. **Public (signed out):** "What is this, and is it for me?" Home is the Flora and Fauna dashboard with the sign-up card; the designer decided the card changes with the tab and always offers an account (`/proto/public-user`). Features reduce, the shell does not change (3.4).
    2. **Registered:** "Where is what I put in up to, and what do I do next?" A personal tracker: DLA requests, nominations, datasets, projects, adding data as the recurring action (`ref-shell.md`).
    3. **BioData Admin:** "What is waiting for me, how much, and in what order?" Operational queues at scale: user access requests, DLA, nominations, flagged concepts.
  - **Principles at stake (0.10):** 4 Tell the truth (a heading names who is acting), 2 One thing first, 1 Say less, 6 Reveal in steps (a count, then the item), 5 Make it obvious (what is this). Where they pull: Say less against Make it obvious for the visitor (an invitation is a sentence that explains nothing the data does not).
  - **What was searched (Mobbin web, 10 searches, 53 screens and 6 sections read):** a visitor to an open data portal, a nature community's signed-out home, a "my submissions with status" home, an application tracker with steps and a next step, an admin home with queue counts, a moderation inbox, a public dataset catalogue, a returning user's home with "continue", a hero with headline statistics and a sign-up. **Limits, so the findings are not read as more than they are:** Mobbin has no government portal and no citizen-science or biodiversity app (a search for iNaturalist returned unrelated products); the matches are SaaS and consumer products, so what transfers is structure, not subject. The closest to a signed-out visitor are discovery products (travel, trails), not data portals.
