# 2026-10-25 - The photo credit's tooltip stays inside the card and reads in two lines

- **Oct 25 2026: The credit tooltip on a species photo no longer spills off the card, and no longer reads as one bold run.** The designer, with a screenshot of the Wombat's summary card (the tooltip reached out to the left of the card over the map, "Photo: c michael hogan, CC BY-" with "NC, Atlas of Living Australia" on the next line): "should exist within the popup, not overflow like this, also the text treatment on the tooltip is awful, please let's fix." This corrects `2026-10-25-10`, which put the credit in `ButtonUtility`'s own tooltip (one string, opened to the left of the icon).
  - **What was wrong, seen in the screenshot and in the code:** (1) the tooltip opened to the left of the icon (`placement="left"`), which put most of a 320px-wide box outside a 320px card; (2) it was one sentence in the tooltip's bold white, so the licence "CC BY-NC" broke at its hyphen and the creator, the licence and the source had no order. `ButtonUtility`'s `tooltip` takes a string only, so it could not use the design system tooltip's two-line form.
  - **Principles (0.10):** 2 One thing first (the creator leads, the licence and source recede), 1 Say less (nothing outside the photo), 9 Craft shows (a line break that cuts a licence name is a defect), 3 One system (the design system's own two-line tooltip, not a variant).
  - **The fix, in `PhotoCredit` (`species-photo-carousel.tsx`):** the `Tooltip` is used directly around the `ButtonUtility` (no `tooltip` prop on it), with `title` "Photo: c michael hogan" (the tooltip's 12/600 white) and `description` "CC BY-NC, Atlas of Living Australia" (its 12/500 supporting colour, gray-400), the licence in a `whitespace-nowrap` span so it never breaks. It opens at `placement="top end"` with `crossOffset={0}` (the design system's -12 is there to balance an arrow, and there is none), so it sits over the photo, above the icon, with its right edge on the icon's. The type is the tooltip's own (2.9: the design system, first); nothing was restyled. The accessible name is unchanged: the whole sentence and "(opens the photo's page)". The hit area and the link are unchanged.
  - **Measured live, for every species card that has a photo in Explore (Playwright, scrollbars drawn):** the tooltip's box against the card's box, for *Lasiorhinus latifrons*, *Leipoa ocellata*, *Polytelis anthopeplus*, *Pseudomys shortridgei*, *Eucalyptus leucoxylon*, *Santalum acuminatum*, *Grevillea lavandulacea* and *Isoodon obesulus* (all four of its photos): every tooltip is inside the card, the box 184 to 204px wide and 60px tall, 9px in from the card's edge (before: it reached out of the card on the left, over the map). Six of the fourteen species with a photo were not reached: five are not in the list for the parks chosen (*Sternula nereis*, *Pandion haliaetus*, *Tiliqua adelaidensis*, *Petrogale xanthopus*, *Xanthorrhoea semiplana*) and the Echidna's row opened no card (below). The longest credit, "Photo: Robert Browne-Cooper", fits. Two lines every time, the licence on one. Zero console errors. The screenshot of the Wombat's card was read.
  - **Not checked:** the Echidna's card (its row in my run opened no summary card, so the credit was not reached; not looked into, and the credit code does not run until a card is open), touch, Safari and Firefox, and a card in a window so short that the photo is under 120px (the tooltip is 60px tall over a photo of at least 168px).
  - **Docs (1.3):** `/principles/craft` has a new detail, "A credit in a tooltip: two lines, one licence, inside the card", with the rule about measuring against the card for every species; `/components/carousel` and `/patterns/map-list-card` already describe the icon and the tooltip and are still true.
  - **Open, for the designer:** (1) whether `ButtonUtility`'s `tooltip` should take a description too (an opt-in prop, 1.6), so the next icon button that needs two lines does not have to wrap itself in `Tooltip`; (2) the touch gap from `-10` is unchanged: a tooltip does not open on a tap.
