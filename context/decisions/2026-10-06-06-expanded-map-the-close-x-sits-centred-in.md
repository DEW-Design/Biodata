# 2026-10-06 - Expanded map: the close X sits centred in its title bar

- **Oct 6 2026: on the project page, the expanded map's close X was cut by the title bar's divider. From the designer's screenshot of the expanded "Adelaide Hills Bushland Survey · geographic extent" map: "x is getting cut off, the icon button. Please fix."**
  - **Cause (measured, 1708x1024):** the shared `ExpandableMap` (`app/pages/_shared/map-search/expandable-map.tsx`) put its close button where every dialog puts it (`top-3`, 12px from the top, as in `modal.tsx`), but its title bar is a single line with a divider under it and was 45px tall. The button is 36px, so it ran from 16px to 52px inside a bar that ended at 45px: 7px over the divider.
  - **Fix, once, in the shared component (1.3):** the title bar is now at least 56px tall (`min-h-14`, with `py-2`, so a bar holding draw buttons can still grow) and the X is 10px from the top (`top-2.5`), so a 36px button is centred in a 56px bar. Measured after: bar 32 to 88, X 42 to 78, 10px clear above and below. Every map that expands (the project's extent, a record's location, a nomination's areas, the DLA "Add a location" takeover) shares this bar, so all of them are fixed together. The corner X of the other dialogs (`modal.tsx`, the walkthrough) is unchanged because they have no title bar and divider under it.
  - **Verified live (Playwright, 1708x1024, BioData Admin, `/pages/project-detail`):** the expanded map's X is whole and centred in the bar, the divider clear of it; zero console errors. `tsc`, eslint and `check:contracts` pass.
  - **Not done:** the takeover presentation (the DLA "Add a location" map) and a record's expanded map were not opened; they use the same bar and X, so the same measurement holds, but they were not looked at. A narrow window was not tried. The round black button over the map's bottom-right corner in the designer's screenshot is the dev-tools launcher of the running app, not part of the map.
  - **Open:** this is the first fix of this kind (0.8), so no `AUTO` check; one is due if a title-bar-plus-corner-button overlap recurs.
