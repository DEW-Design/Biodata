# 2026-10-26 - Sunset the other options: nominations, voucher, taxonomy and notification management

- **Oct 26 2026: four screens that were being compared are decided, and the options not chosen are deleted (CONTRACTS 4.4: "When a direction is chosen the others are deleted").** The designer: "Let's keep option 2 for Nomination sensitive species. Let's keep option 1 for voucher mgmt. Keep option 2 for taxonomy mgmt. Keep option 2 for notification mgmt. Sunset the other options."
  - **How "option 2" was read (0.4, for the designer to correct).** Nominations and Taxonomy Management were compared through the Version tool, not the Layout tool, and their options were labelled "Version 1" and "Version 3" (nominations; version 2 had been deleted on 14 Oct 2026) and "Version 1" and "Version 2" (taxonomy). "Option 2" was taken as the second of the two on each: nominations kept version 3 (species and locations, admins approve, edit and rate), taxonomy kept version 2 (every rank, each change on one page). Notification Management and Voucher Management were compared through the Layout tool, labelled Option 1 and Option 2, and kept as said: notifications Option 2, vouchers Option 1.
  - **The kept option is now the screen, at the screen's own route.** Where the kept option lived at a second route, it was moved to the first, so the rail, the Add menu, column 2, Home and every link still reach it, and nothing says "version" or "option" any more. Redirects were not added (the site is a static export and the old routes were never linked from the product).

    About 2,100 lines went and about 470 were added across 63 files. Each area's roots are now one constant (`NOMINATION_ROOT`, `VM_ROOT`, `TX_ROOT`, `NT_ROOT`) instead of a React context, and `TX_V2_ROOT` is gone. `nomination-record-v3.tsx` is `nomination-record.tsx` (`NominationRecord`), and it now holds `NominationNotFound`, which lived in the deleted record page.
  - **Links that pointed into a retired option, and where they go.** The species record's Rename, Combine and Split (menu on its card) went to the guided change, `/pages/taxonomy/new?type=...&from=<id>`; they now open that change's own page, on the species' kingdom (`/pages/taxonomy/rename?kingdom=Flora`). The superseded-taxon notice's "Open <successor>" went to `/pages/taxonomy/<id>`; it now opens the successor's page at `/record?id=...`. The voucher comparison's "Add taxon" went to the guided Append; it opens `/pages/taxonomy/append` on the kingdom of BioData's current taxon. Update taxonomy's menu and the species list's rows were already version 2's. The post-6-oct lab embedded the species-only list and the table list: it now shows the list of every rank (`HierarchyList`) and the notifications list that stayed (`NtList`). The `/pages` index (`screen-index.ts`) lost its nine retired entries and its kept ones were renamed without "version" or "Option" and re-pathed (a taxon page uses the Blue Gum's path of names).
  - **What a person can no longer do, because it was only in a retired option (stated so it is a decision, not a surprise).**
    1. Notifications: the Settings tab's label and value cards are gone from a notification's page. The Overview's How it works says when, who, what and then, but it does not show the delay after an event, the start and end dates of a schedule, the category, or each attachment's size. They are still in the edit form.
    2. Taxonomy: starting a Rename, Combine or Split from a species' page no longer carries the species into the form: the one-page forms start empty on the species' kingdom and the taxon is picked in the form (the guided change started from the species). If the designer wants the taxon carried, the forms need a `from` they do not have.
    3. Taxonomy: the species-only list and its Species count in column 2 are gone, and so is "All species" as a name (Back links and the superseded notice say "All taxa").
    4. Nominations: the species-only list and record page are gone (the list shows species and locations on two tabs, and a record has Nomination and Audit history), and the one "Nominate a new species" button is the Nominate menu (Sensitive species, Sensitive location).
    5. Vouchers: the grid with a row per concept and red and amber lines is gone.
  - **Found on the way, fixed (not part of the sunset).** `useVouchersHydrated` did not load the saved decisions the way every other store's hook does, so a batch or record opened by its address (a refresh, a pasted link) drew nothing, with no error, and only worked after the list had been opened. It now rehydrates first, like `useDlasHydrated` and the others. This is a sibling fix of an existing fault (0.7), found in the live pass, and it is why `/pages/vouchers/<batch>` shows its table on a first load.
  - **Kept, on purpose.** `LayoutOptionSwitcher` (still used by the biodata home page and three labs) and `VersionSwitcher` (`version-switcher.tsx`, now used by no screen): they are the tools 4.4 names for the next comparison, and deleting a shared tool is a separate decision. The generic `formSidebar` option of the taxonomy shell and the old default `actionHref` helper's shape were left as small unused hooks. The Project registration page's "option-2" folder and the BioData home's "version-2" are other things (the form's route and the original home page) and are untouched. Earlier decision files that describe the retired routes are history and are not edited.
