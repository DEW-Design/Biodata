# 2026-11-10 - Taxonomy Management: a read-only Sensitive species risk tab on a species

- **Nov 10 2026: a Sensitive species risk tab on a species, read only.** The designer: "When I click on a particular species, I want to see a new tab called Sensitive species risk on the tabs. It could be right after overview. Feature: adding a new tab on the taxa / species to manage sensitive species risk and distribution levels." Asked first what "manage" meant, because the rating is not stored per species (a species' rating is made from its accepted nominations, `currentSpeciesRating`) and a separate Species sensitivity screen was removed on 12 Oct for that reason (`2026-10-12-09`). The designer: "they're read only on the taxonomy management species screen - information is inherited from the species nomination screens. If they want to edit, they'll have to go back to the species nomination screen to edit." So there is one source of truth, the nominations, and the tab only reads it. "Distribution levels" is read as the nominations' User access level (Level 1 Public to Level 4 Admin only), beside the Data release risk.
  Job and principles first (0.10): a BioData Admin or Super Admin, opening a species in Taxonomy Management, sees how sensitive it is and where that comes from, and can reach the nomination to change it. 4 Tell the truth (the rating is the nominations' own, not a copy), 1 Say less (one sentence of explanation, said once), 5 Make it obvious (each nomination one press away, "Nominate this species" when there is none), 7 Design every state, 3 One system. Closest pages: the nomination form's species step (`ExistingRestrictionsTable`, the species' current rating) and the nomination record's rating values; Taxonomy's own `Card` and label-over-value facts.
  **Built.** `tx-sensitivity.tsx` (new): the tab. `tx-records.tsx`: on a species record the tabs are the kingdom's own with this one inserted after the first (Main, the designer's "overview"), for Flora and Fauna alike; it is read only, so editing a species ("Edit species") leaves it as it is (no inputs in it). It has: a "Read only" notice (only when a rating is in force); "Current rating" (whole species: Applies to "All occurrence records", the risk badge with its treatment, the access level and who sees records as held; by attribute: "Subset of occurrence records" and a table of Attribute, Data release risk and User access level, then "Every other attribute: Negligible, Level 1 - Public"); and "Nominations", the existing-restrictions table with each nomination's id now a link to it (`ExistingRestrictionsTable` takes an opt-in `hrefFor`; without it the nomination form's table is as it was). Icon `ShieldTick`, new to the product, added to CONTRACTS 3.13's list as that clause asks (the clause's scope file regenerated; `Shield01` is the nomination's What to protect).
  **States.** In force (accepted, not removed by a later one): the rating and the table. Only open nominations (submitted, under review, returned): "Not rated", "A nomination is with the panel. The rating is set when one is accepted. Until then its records are released as held. Negligible risk, Level 1 - Public." and the table, with no Nominate button. None: "Not rated", "No accepted nomination sets a rating for this species, so its records are released as held." and "Nominate this species", which opens a new nomination on that species: `/pages/nominations/new?species=<scientific name>` (new, additive: `NominationForm` takes `initialSpeciesId`, ignored when it does not match a species or the nomination already exists). A nomination is for a species by its scientific name, so that is how the tab finds its nominations. Wording is new copy, proposed.
  **Not on a higher taxon or an infraspecies.** A genus, family and so on have no rating of their own; an infraspecies page (Isoodon obesulus obesulus) is a higher-record page and has no tab. Whether an infraspecies should show its species' rating is open.
  Verified: `tsc`, `eslint` on `app/pages/_shared/taxonomy`, `_shared/nominations` and `app/pages/nominations`, and `check:contracts` pass. Live at 1440x900 with scrollbars drawn, zero console errors, as BioData Admin and Super Admin: the tab is second on a Flora species and a Fauna species, right after Main; Southern Brown Bandicoot (NSS-2026-00007, accepted, by attribute) shows the attribute table, Low, Level 2 - DLA and the Observer rule, with the Read only notice and the nomination's id as a link that opens it with the role kept; Short-beaked Echidna and the Blue Gum show Not rated and the Nominate link, which opens the new nomination with the species chosen; after a registered user submits one, the tab shows the panel line and the Submitted row and no button; after the Super Admin starts the review, sets High and accepts, it shows All occurrence records, High (Embargoed or withheld data) and Level 3 - Government with who sees it as held; editing a species leaves the tab with no inputs; a genus has no tab. Typography (2.9): no new layer; the card, label and value facts, table, badge, notice and buttons are the ones the taxonomy and nomination pages already use. Not checked: a narrow window, a species with several accepted nominations merged (the merge is `currentSpeciesRating`'s, unchanged), touch emulation.
