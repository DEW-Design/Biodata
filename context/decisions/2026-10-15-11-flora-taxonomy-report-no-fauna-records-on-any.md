# 2026-10-15 - Flora Taxonomy Report: no Fauna records on any tab

- **Oct 15 2026: no Fauna on the Flora Taxonomy Report.** The designer, off a screenshot of the report's hero showing Flora 2 and Fauna 5: "there needs to be no fauna records on the flora taxonomy report, please fix." This settles open item 1 of `2026-10-15-08-*` (should the report be Flora only) and the Fauna half of the area tabs' question in `-06-*` and `-07-*`.

  **What changed.** Every tab now reads through two hooks (`taxonomy-flora.ts`) and never through `useTaxa` and `useChanges`: `useFloraTaxa` (the taxa whose kingdom is Flora) and `useFloraChanges` (the changes in the log, without any that touched a Fauna taxon, as an input or an output; a change whose result is no longer held is kept, as it said what was done). So a Fauna taxon is in none of the ten tabs, in no Rename, Split or Append row, in no audit log event and in no synonym row. The first tab's hero no longer counts Flora and Fauna: it shows **Families** and **Genera** (the distinct ones in view), and the Kingdom attribute left its Filter menu, there being one kingdom.
  - **Group.** The sheet's Group parameter was the species group (Plants, Mammals, Birds ...), which is "Plants" for every row now. It reads the Flora taxon's **Major group** instead (the sheet's own "Major Group" column; Flora's Main has it), so Group and the Major group column say the same thing. The sample taxa hold no Major group, so the Group list has only All until taxa have one. This was the open question in `-05-*` (Group as the species group or the Major group field); say if it should be the other.
  - **Kept on purpose:** the Fauna fields in the row builders (Fauna's Non Synonym?, Indigenous?, Regional Data, Australian Distribution, Subspecific, Infraspecific, its synonyms' creation and modified dates) are not removed: they are unreachable here and are for the Fauna report that is to follow this one.

  **What the sample data now shows** (a fresh browser; the sample taxa are 2 Flora and 5 Fauna): List detail 2 rows; SA regions 5; Aus / World 5; Reg herb data 3; Audit log 4 (each Flora taxon's created and edited days); Species attributes, Rename, Split, Append and **Species synonym** are empty. Species synonym was 5 rows, all of them Fauna taxa's: the two Flora sample taxa have no synonyms, and none is made up. The Taxonomy Management screens are unchanged and still show all seven taxa.
