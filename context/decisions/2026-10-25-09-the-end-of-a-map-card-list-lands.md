# 2026-10-25 - The end of a map-card list lands quietly: no jump, the button and the fade ease away

- **Oct 25 2026: Reaching the end of a map-card list (Explore's results, Map visualise's errors) no longer throws the rows, and the button and the fade ease away instead of vanishing in one frame.** The designer, with a screen recording of Map visualise, pressing "Show 1 more" down to the last row: "after show one more, the transition to the end of the list looks awful, can you please fix? When the fix is made, let's make sure it shows up consistently across." (`/refine-prompt`.) One component, so every list built from it has the fix: `MapResultList` (`app/pages/_shared/map-search/result-list.tsx`) and `ScrollFade` (`scroll-fade.tsx`), used by Explore, Map visualise and `/patterns/map-list-card`.
  - **What was wrong (measured by reproducing the recording, a 740px window, the last press):** while the button is up the list keeps 68px of room under its last row (so the last row can scroll clear of the button); when the last row cleared the button, three things changed in one frame: the button was removed, the fade was removed, and the room collapsed to 12px, which clamped the scroll position (8052 to 8012) so the rows jumped about 40px and the smooth scroll was cut off. A wheel that scrolled into that room was clamped the same way (8066 to 8012 in a frame). In the recording's last frames the button comes back after it had gone; scrolling up from the end does that (the last row is cut again, so the button returns), and it came back with the same pop, which is now eased. Why the list moved up in the recording is not known from the video.
  - **Terms (the vocabulary skills):** layout shift (the rows moving as the padding collapsed), easing and transition property (opacity and the fade's length, never a layout property someone sees move), duration (150ms for the button, 200ms for the fade).
  - **Principles (0.10):** 9 Craft shows (a detail made and measured), 3 One system (the fix is made once, in the component, so Explore and Map visualise cannot differ), 5 Make it obvious (the button still says there is more until the end is in view, and goes when it is), 6 Reveal in steps (a fade says there is more, and now also says when there is not).
  - **The fix:**
    1. **The end is where the last row has its 12px,** read from the rows and the padding as it is this frame (`restEnd`), not from the button's state. The button goes when the scroll gets there, and a press never scrolls further than that (`scrollToRow` is clamped to it), so taking the room away moves nothing. A press at the end now needs no extra "Show 1 more" (at a 700px window the last press was "Show 1" before and the sequence now ends at "Show 3").
    2. **The room under the last row eases** from 68 to 12px over 200ms (`scroll-fade-ease`, `app/globals.css`), so a wheel that overshot the end settles over about 12 frames instead of being cut back in one.
    3. **The button stays mounted, inert and invisible, while it fades** (150ms: ease-in as it goes, ease-out as it returns), and keeps its last label ("Show 1 more", never "Show 0 more").
    4. **The fade eases:** its length and its clear zone are registered lengths (`@property --scroll-fade-length`, `--scroll-fade-clear`) that go to 0 over 200ms (ease-out) and the mask comes off when they have. It applies to every `ScrollFade`, so the tab strips ease at their right edge too (measured on Map visualise's tabs: 64px to 0 and the mask off by 580ms). `MapResultList` now asks for no fade (`fade={more ? FADE : 0}`) when there is no button, where before a few pixels of room left the fade on with no button.
  - **A bug the fix made and the measurement caught:** with the room easing, "more" was read from a `scrollHeight` that was still catching up, so after a small scroll up from the end the button flipped on and off every frame (inert true, false, true) for as long as it was watched. Reading the end from the rows and the live padding, not from the state, removed it. The code before this change had no such loop (its geometry changed in one frame); it was introduced and removed within this task.
  - **Measured live (Playwright, scrollbars drawn, `requestAnimationFrame` sampling):** Map visualise pressed through to the end at windows 700, 740, 780, 860, 968 and 1080px high: no backward scroll step in any press, and every list ends with the last row 12px above the foot, padding 12px, the button at opacity 0 and inert, the mask off. The final press at 740: the scroll runs 7984 to 8012 and stops; then the button goes 1.00 to 0.00 and the fade 120px to 0 over about 200ms, the padding 68 to 12, and nothing moves. A wheel to the end: the overshoot of 54px settles in 12 frames. Scrolling up 30px from the end: the button returns eased (opacity 0 to 1 over about 200ms, padding 12 to 68), no flicker. Explore (6 parks, 9 species) at 700, 800 and 968px: same end state, no backward step. `/patterns/map-list-card`: presses 25, 6, 2 and ends. A keyboard press (Enter on the button) moved focus to a row at every step including the last. Reduced motion: no transitions, the same end state. Zero console errors in all of it.
  - **Not checked:** touch and a real trackpad's momentum (the wheel was Playwright's, in steps of 90px), Safari and Firefox (the easing needs `@property`, Safari 16.4 and Firefox 128; without it the fade snaps as it did and nothing else changes), a screen reader on the inert button, a list of 98 rows in Explore (its search returns 9).
  - **Docs corrected (1.3):** `/patterns/map-list-card` (the fade eases; the button goes when the last row has its 12px; a new bullet, "The end lands quietly") and `/principles/craft` (a new detail under Scroll and fade, with the rule about reading the end from the rows and not from a state that easing lags behind). The comments of `result-list.tsx` and `scroll-fade.tsx` say the same.
  - **Not changed, and why:** "Show N more results" in the header search's dropdown (`global-search.tsx`) is a row in a menu that adds rows, with no pinned button, no fade and no room to collapse, so it has no end to land; it stays as it is. The 12px greys on the Species tab are from the entry before this one.
  - **Where the behaviour lives (0.6 item 9):** in `MapResultList` and `ScrollFade`, where it already was, in `app/pages/_shared/map-search`; nothing new was built, and the open 1.10 item (move both into `components/**` with an override) is unchanged: `2026-10-23-70`. The easing's two registered lengths and `scroll-fade-ease` are in `app/globals.css` next to the other utilities.
  - **Open, for the designer:** the resting end keeps 12px under the last row (as a list that never had the button does); the alternative that moves nothing at all is to keep the 68px under the last row for good, at the cost of 56px of air at the foot of every list that has been through "Show more". The recommendation is the 12px. Whether the same easing should be taken off the tab strips (they have it now, and it is the same component) is also the designer's to say.
