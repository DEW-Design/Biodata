Through `/refine-prompt`: the "wash gradient" is the `ScrollFade` mask on the tabs (a 64px `mask-image` that shows only while there is more to scroll to), so the list gets the same.
  - **Changed (`scroll-fade.tsx`, `dataset-error-map.tsx`):**
    - **`ScrollFade` has a vertical axis** (`axis="y"`, opt-in; the default is the horizontal strip, so the tabs here and in Explore are unchanged, 1.6) and a `scrollRef` to its scrolling element (the list scrolls itself with `scrollTo` and reads its rows). The fade is the same mask over the same 64px, at the bottom edge, on while the list can scroll further (it watches scrolling, resizing and rows being added or taken away), off at the end and when everything fits.
    - **The list is the scroller, the button is outside it.** A sticky button inside a masked scroller would be faded with the rows, so "Show N more" now sits over the list's foot (`absolute`, 12px from the bottom, above the list), and the list is padded 64px at its foot while the button is there, so the last row scrolls clear of it (measured: 16px between the last row and the button at the end), and 12px otherwise. The reveal (the new rows scrolled into view, focus to the first new row for a keyboard press) reads the same rows.
    - **The button stays the secondary `Button`, on a lifted wrapper.** Its visibility is raised by elevation and a clean surface, not by changing the button: a wrapper carries the card's largest shadow token (`shadow-2xl`) so it lifts off the faded rows, and the rows behind it are fading out. Its look, colour, size and the 44px hit area (`before:-inset-y-1 before:inset-x-0`, as it was) are unchanged; measured, a press 3px above or below it lands on it and 8px does not. A primary version was tried first (a brand fill, 7.3:1) and the designer rejected it ("shadow 2xl button was fine, we don't need a primary variant, there was no need to change the design of the button altogether"), so the button's design is not touched. The secondary's gray-300 ring is about 1.5:1 against white, under the 3:1 for a component's edge; its label is 7.5:1, and that is left as the design system has it.
