# 2026-10-02 - Audit Log tab: one shared AuditLog, a sentence per status move (Vercel activity feed)

- **Oct 2 2026: Audit Log tab: one shared AuditLog, a sentence per status move (Vercel activity feed).** The designer asked for audit-log patterns from Mobbin, pointed at Vercel's, and agreed the recommendation (a sentence feed, a Current chip, runs by the same person under one header, the note under its move; filters, export, expandable rows, before and after values and from-to chips held back).
  - **Built:** `app/pages/_shared/audit-log.tsx` (`AuditLog`). One sentence per move, newest first: a small avatar and the person's name ("System" with a bolt for a move the clock made), "moved this to" (or "created this as" for a draft), the status `Badge`, and the date at the right (Today, Yesterday, then "23 Sep 2026" in the app's own three-letter months). The newest move has a "Current" chip. Consecutive moves by one person share a header and the lines under it drop the name. A note sits under its move. An empty log says so. It replaces three hand-built lists of `RecordRow`s: DLA, DSA and the nomination's Audit history, which had the same shape and so now read the same (1.3).
  - **Not built, as agreed:** month headings (a record has a handful of moves), filters and export, expandable rows, before and after values, from-to chips. They belong to a cross-record audit report, if one is added.
  - **Typography sources:** the sentence is `text-sm text-secondary` like the `RecordRow` label, the person `font-medium text-primary` like the record values on the DLA page, the date `text-sm text-tertiary` with tabular figures; measured live as Barlow 14px/20px at weights 400, 500 and 400, no new layer.
  - **Contract and docs:** CONTRACTS.md 4.6 item 6 and its "MUST NOT build a second audit log" line (rules regenerated); a pattern page at `/patterns/audit-log` (anatomy, a typical trail, a note and a system move, recent moves, empty, rules, held back, Figma "not linked yet"), listed in `lib/nav.ts` and the README.
  - **Verified:** `tsc`, `eslint` on the touched files, `check:contracts`, a production build; live browser on DLA, DSA (including a rejection note and a rebuilt legacy trail), a nomination and the pattern page: sentences, Current chip, same-person headers, System with the bolt, Today and Yesterday, zero console errors.
  - **Open:** dates only, so no times (the pattern's relative "3m" has no equivalent); not tested at narrow widths or with a keyboard (the log has no interactive controls). Not committed.
