- **Sept 25 2026: Explore option 3 treats search areas as layers, and adding one is one menu, per designer direction ("we'll have to treat multiple points as layers ... the experience needs to be tightened up for adding multiple points / shapefile / choosing an area").** Built on option 3 only (`float-grow`); options 1, 2, 4 and 5 keep their controls.
  - **Areas list (column 2, `map-search/area-layers.tsx`, page-shared):** one row per area you added (a drawn circle or polygon, an entered point, each selected national park, each shapefile). A row has a show/hide toggle, a name ("Circle 1", "Point 1", the park's name, the file name), what it is ("25 km radius", "Shapefile · 2 points") with its record count, and a "..." menu (Rename, Change radius, Remove). Pointing at a row draws that shape heavier on the map; clicking the name zooms to it. "Clear all" appears with more than one. **Unlike a design tool, order means nothing:** the search is the union of the visible areas, so rows are not stacked or reordered. A hidden area is neither drawn nor searched; if every area is hidden the card says so. Names are given when an area is added, so removing one never renumbers the others. Radius is per area (a park no longer shares one radius). A keyword with no area at all still searches the whole state and shows an "All of South Australia" row with no toggle, removed by clearing the word.
  - **Counts:** each row counts records the way the results do (spatial, keyword, published projects only, plus the projects those records belong to), so a single area's number equals the panel total when it is the only area (checked: Belair 26, Flinders Chase 12, together 38). Overlapping areas can sum to more than the total, which is honest for a union.
  - **Adding an area:** the four method tabs are gone from the card. One "Add area" menu (Draw a circle, Draw a polygon, Enter coordinates, Choose national parks, Upload a shapefile) opens a short flow for that method only, which closes when the area is added (Choose national parks stays open until Done, since it takes several). The search field is now the real `Input` with just the keyword, no chips (the areas list is beside it, so chips would state the same fact twice). Empty state says what to do, once, in the card.
  - **Shared changes, additive:** `SAMap` gained `highlightedBoundaryIds` and `fitRequest` (zoom to one area, same padding rules as the main fit). The coordinate and park radius fields (`InputNumber` with `minValue` 1 and `step` 5) snapped 15 to 16 and 25 to 26 on blur while the state kept 15 and 25, so the field and the search disagreed; `minValue` is now 5 so the steps line up (also fixes option 1).
  - **Verified live on option 3:** two parks give 2 rows with counts and the right total; draw a circle (panel opens, closes when drawn, gets "Circle 1"); enter coordinates ("Point 1"); hide (total drops to the visible area); rename; change radius (25 to 60 km, row updates); remove; upload a GeoJSON (one row "pts.geojson · 2 points", panel closes); hover thickens the shape (stroke 2 to 4); zoom to area changes the scale from 100 km to 10 km; zero console errors; `tsc`, `eslint`, `check:contracts` clean.
  - **Noted, not built (per designer): "exclude" areas.** A layer that removes its records from the union (drawing a hole in a search) is the most layer-like feature and changes the filtering logic. Backlog: revisit if a stakeholder asks for it.
